Most dining table moves in [[METRO]] cost between $26 and $95, depending on distance — [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile.

## The formula

[[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile. A dining table doesn't add its own fee.

| 3 miles | $26.30 | $43.30 |
| 10 miles | $48.00 | $65.00 |
| 25 miles | $94.50 | $111.50 |

## Table specs

Most dining tables fit [[TIER_2]]. Let the driver know your chair count when you book — chairs travel in the same haul and affect how much room is left.

## Leg removal

If the legs detach, remove them before pickup — easier to load, less risk of damage, and more room for chairs in the same trip.

## Glass tops

Glass tabletops are wrapped and secured separately from the base for the drive.

## What to prep

Remove detachable legs, wrap glass tops separately, and count your chairs before booking.

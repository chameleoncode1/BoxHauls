Photograph the damage immediately, then file a claim in the app — here's the full process.

## Photograph it first

Before moving or attempting to fix anything, photograph the damage and the item's overall condition. Clear photos are the most important part of a claim.

## File the claim

Open the haul in the app and start a claim with your photos and a short description of what happened.

## What happens next

BoxHauls reviews the claim against the driver's own load photos and the insurance policy covering the haul. See [what happens if something is damaged](/trust/damage-claims/) for the full timeline and who pays.

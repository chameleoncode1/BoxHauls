Moving a washer and dryer safely means draining the washer, securing the drum, and leaving hookups to a licensed pro.

[PHOTO: washer and dryer with hoses disconnected and coiled, ready for transport]

## Disconnect and drain

Disconnect hoses and cords, and drain any remaining water from the washer before the move.

## Install shipping bolts

If you have the original shipping bolts, install them — they lock the drum in place and prevent internal damage during transport.

## Secure loose parts

Tape the dryer door shut, and secure or remove a loose lint trap.

## Load and strap

Load both units and strap them securely against the truck wall so they can't shift during the drive.

## Reconnect carefully

At the destination, reconnect hoses and cords yourself if you're comfortable, but leave gas or vent connections to a licensed installer.

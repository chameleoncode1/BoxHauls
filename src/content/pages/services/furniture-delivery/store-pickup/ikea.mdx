[[METRO]] doesn't have its own IKEA — the nearest store is roughly [[LOCAL_ENTITIES.no_ikea_nearest_hours]] hours away, so a standard local BoxHauls haul to that store isn't practical. Here's what actually works.

## No local store

IKEA hasn't built a store in the [[METRO]] area. A round trip to the nearest location is a multi-hour drive each way, well outside a normal local haul.

## What does work

If you've shipped an IKEA order to a local address, a nearby pickup locker, or had a friend bring it back from a trip to the nearest store, BoxHauls can move it from there to your home — same as any other flat-pack furniture delivery.

## Flat-pack, not assembled

IKEA furniture ships flat-pack (boxed, unassembled). BoxHauls delivers it as-is — assembly is a separate job, not part of the haul.

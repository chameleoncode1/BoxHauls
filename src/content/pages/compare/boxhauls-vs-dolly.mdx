Dolly is in the middle of becoming a different company — it was acquired by Taskrabbit and is rebranding to "Taskrabbit Delivery," and its published coverage doesn't clearly include [[METRO]].

## A brand in transition

As of August 2026, Dolly's own site increasingly redirects into Taskrabbit's booking flow under the "Taskrabbit Delivery" name. Anything below describes Dolly's historical model, which may not hold once the rebrand finishes.

## Coverage

Dolly/Taskrabbit Delivery's published California markets include Sacramento, San Diego, San Francisco, San Jose, Los Angeles, and Orange County — [[METRO]] was not found in that published list as of August 2026.

## Where Dolly wins

Dolly's hourly model can work out cheaper for a large job with a lot of stops, and it's part of a much larger national marketplace.

## Where BoxHauls wins

A confirmed, dedicated local presence in [[METRO]] and [[NEIGHBORHOOD]], and a flat per-trip price shown before you book — no hourly clock, no dependence on whether a Helper happens to accept your job.

## Verdict

If you're in [[METRO]], check Dolly/Taskrabbit Delivery's current coverage directly before counting on it — [BoxHauls](/pricing/) is confirmed to operate here today.

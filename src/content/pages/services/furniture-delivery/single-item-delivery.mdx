No minimum order — BoxHauls delivers one item just as readily as a full load, at the same [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile.

## The no-minimum promise

Whether it's one box or a full apartment, BoxHauls prices the same way: base fare plus mileage, plus any item or helper fee that applies. There's no minimum charge for a small order.

## Typical single items

A dresser, a bookshelf, a desk, a single box too large to ship, or one piece bought from a store or Marketplace seller.

## Price example

A single item delivered 10 miles runs about $48 before any add-on fees — see [pricing](/pricing/) for the full formula and worked examples at other distances.

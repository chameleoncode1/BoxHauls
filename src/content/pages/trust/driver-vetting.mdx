Every BoxHauls driver clears six checks before their first haul, run through [[BACKGROUND_PROVIDER]]:

1. **Identity** — government ID verification.
2. **License** — valid driver's license, checked against the name on the account.
3. **Driving record** — moving violations and at-fault accidents reviewed.
4. **Criminal background** — a [[BACKGROUND_PROVIDER]] background check.
5. **Vehicle inspection** — the truck itself is checked against the [tier requirements](/drive/requirements/).
6. **Insurance verification** — the driver's own auto insurance is confirmed active.

## Staying active

Re-check cadence: [[RECHECK_CADENCE]]. Drivers whose rating falls below [[RATING_THRESHOLD]] are removed from active status.

See the [trust overview](/trust/) for insurance coverage and the damage-claim process, or [driver requirements](/drive/requirements/) for the equipment and vehicle standards checked in step 5.

Piano moving depends heavily on the type — here's the honest limit.

## Uprights, maybe

An upright piano may be possible with [[TIER_3]] and a helper, depending on the specific driver's equipment and experience moving pianos. Ask before booking rather than assuming — not every driver is set up for it.

## Grands, no

Grand pianos need specialized rigging, a piano board, and a trained moving crew — well beyond what a truck-and-driver haul is built for. BoxHauls refers grand piano moves to a specialized piano mover rather than attempting them.

## The formula, for uprights

If a driver can take your upright, pricing follows the standard formula: [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile, plus a helper fee.

## What to prep

Confirm with the driver ahead of time that they're equipped for an upright piano, and clear a path with no tight turns.

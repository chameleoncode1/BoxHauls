## Dump runs and transfer stations

Fresno-area dump runs go to one of two places, depending on load and route:

- **American Avenue Disposal Site** — the Fresno County landfill near Kerman. [[LANDFILL_HOURS_FEES]]
- **Cedar Avenue Recycling & Transfer Station (CARTS)**, Fresno — the closer option for many in-town loads, and a mattress/appliance recycling option. [[CARTS_HOURS_FEES]]

See [dump fees in [[METRO]]](/guides/dump-fees-in-fresno/) for the full current rate breakdown.

## Where drivers pick up most often

In order of how often BoxHauls drivers pick up from each: Costco Clovis, Living Spaces Fresno, Home Depot Clovis, Costco Fresno, Lowe's, and then Ashley Furniture, Big Lots, Walmart, Target, and Mattress Firm. See [store pickup](/services/furniture-delivery/store-pickup/) for how pickup-lane handling works at any retailer.

**No IKEA in Fresno** — the nearest location is roughly [[LOCAL_ENTITIES.no_ikea_nearest_hours]] hours away. If you've shipped an IKEA order to a local pickup point, BoxHauls can still get it home.

## Bulky-item pickup: the gap BoxHauls fills

The City of Fresno runs Operation Clean Up, a scheduled bulky-item pickup by area. [[FRESNO_BULKY_SCHEDULE]] Between scheduled pickups — which is most of the year for any given address — BoxHauls is the same-day option. See [bulky-item pickup in [[METRO]]](/guides/bulky-item-pickup-in-fresno/) for the full breakdown.

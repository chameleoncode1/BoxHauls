A BoxHauls driver loads, secures, drives, and unloads your item — placing it in the first room, not just dropping it at the curb.

## Before pickup

Once a driver accepts your haul, you can message them directly in the app. They'll confirm the pickup address and any details about the item.

## At pickup

The driver checks the item, loads it, and secures it for transport — wrapping furniture and strapping down anything that could shift.

## During the drive

You can track the haul's progress in the app.

## At drop-off

The driver carries the item inside to the first room, not just to the curb, then you confirm the haul is complete.

## Vetting

Every driver clears a background check, license verification, and vehicle inspection before their first haul — see [trust & safety](/trust/) for the full process.

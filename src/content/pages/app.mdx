## What the app does

Enter your addresses, item, and truck tier, see the price, and get matched with a nearby driver — see [how it works](/how-it-works/) for the full flow. Message and track your driver, then pay and rate the haul once it's done.

## Driving

Own a pickup truck? [Drive for BoxHauls](/drive/) uses the same app to accept hauls and get paid.

BoxHauls removes old hot tubs for disposal — whole or cut into sections, depending on size and access.

## Whole vs. cut into sections

A whole hot tub is heavy and awkward to move even empty. Depending on the tub's size and your access path, cutting it into sections can make removal faster and safer — the driver will advise what makes sense for your specific setup.

## Crew size

Hot tub removal takes two people at minimum, given the weight and shape — see [[HELPER_AVAILABILITY_NOTE]]

## Disposal fee

Hot tubs are bulky items and may carry a separate disposal fee at the landfill, passed through at cost the same way any gate fee is.

## Before pickup

Drain the tub completely — a full or partially full hot tub is far too heavy to move safely.

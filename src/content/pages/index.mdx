## Why people book BoxHauls

- **Insured.** Every haul is covered while it's in the truck.
- **Background-checked.** Every driver clears a [[BACKGROUND_PROVIDER]] background check before their first haul.
- **Upfront price.** [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile — see the total before you confirm, not after the truck shows up.

## How it works

1. Enter your pickup and drop-off addresses, pick what's moving and a [truck tier](/pricing/truck-sizes/), and see the price.
2. A nearby driver accepts the haul and heads your way.
3. They load it, secure it, drive it, and unload it at the drop-off. You pay and rate the haul in the app.

Read the [full step-by-step](/how-it-works/) for what happens at each stage, including the payment hold and cancellation window.

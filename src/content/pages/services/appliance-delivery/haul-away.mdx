Getting a new appliance delivered? Book haul-away for the old one in the same trip.

## Refrigerant rules

Refrigerators and freezers contain refrigerant that must be professionally recovered before the unit can be recycled or scrapped — this is a federal requirement, not something a standard dump run handles. BoxHauls routes old refrigeration units to a facility that can process them correctly.

## Utility rebate programs

PG&E and other utilities serving [[METRO]] have offered rebate programs for recycling an old, working refrigerator or freezer. Program terms and availability change — check directly with your utility before booking if a rebate matters to you.

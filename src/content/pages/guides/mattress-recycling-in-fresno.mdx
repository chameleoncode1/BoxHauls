A mattress doesn't have to go straight to the landfill — some transfer stations and specialty recyclers in [[METRO]] accept them separately.

## Why mattresses are handled separately

Mattresses take up significant landfill volume and don't compact well, so many facilities handle them through a separate recycling stream rather than general waste.

## Where they go

Check current options at [[METRO]]'s transfer stations — see [dump fees in [[METRO]]](/guides/dump-fees-in-fresno/) for the two main local options and their current policies.

## Fees

Mattresses can carry a separate handling or recycling fee at some facilities, passed through at cost the same way a standard gate fee is.

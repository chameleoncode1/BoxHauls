Store delivery can be free, but it's usually slower — BoxHauls trades a delivery fee for same-day pickup and placement where you want it.

## Timing

Store delivery windows are frequently scheduled days or even weeks out, especially for furniture. Picking up the same item yourself via BoxHauls the day you buy it skips that wait entirely.

## What's included

Store delivery policies vary widely — some retailers only deliver to the curb or driveway, others bring items inside. BoxHauls includes placement to the first room on every haul, not just curbside drop-off.

## Cost

Store delivery is sometimes free above a purchase minimum, sometimes a flat fee — it depends entirely on the retailer and order size. BoxHauls is a consistent [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]]/mile regardless of what store you're buying from.

## Where store delivery wins

If it's genuinely free and your timeline isn't tight, letting the store handle it costs nothing extra.

## Where BoxHauls wins

Same-day pickup instead of a days-out delivery window, and placement in the room you actually want it — see [store pickup](/services/furniture-delivery/store-pickup/) for how same-day pickup from a retailer works.

If timing matters more than a delivery fee, BoxHauls gets it home the same day — see [pricing](/pricing/).

BoxHauls delivers refrigerators, washers, dryers, and other appliances — with two-person handling standard, since they're heavy and awkward for one person.

## Two-person handling

Add a helper for any appliance haul — most weigh well over 150 lb and are awkward to carry alone through doorways or up stairs.

## Prep checklist

- **Defrost** refrigerators at least 24 hours before pickup.
- **Disconnect** hoses, cords, and vent connections.
- **Strap or tape** washer drums and refrigerator doors shut for transport.

## Stairs and doorways

Measure doorways before booking — some refrigerators need door removal to clear a tight doorway. Add a helper for any stairs.

## Old-unit haul-away

Getting a new appliance delivered? Book haul-away for the old one in the same trip — see [old appliance haul-away and recycling](/services/appliance-delivery/haul-away/).

## What drivers don't do

Drivers deliver and place the appliance, but they don't connect gas lines or water lines — that needs a licensed plumber or electrician. Have your connections ready, or arrange that separately.

## Which tier fits

Most refrigerators and washer/dryer pairs fit [[TIER_2]] or [[TIER_3]] — see [truck sizes](/pricing/truck-sizes/) for exact fit.

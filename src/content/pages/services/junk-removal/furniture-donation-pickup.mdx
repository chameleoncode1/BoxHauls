BoxHauls drops off furniture donations at the charity or donation center you choose.

## Where donations go

You choose the donation site — a thrift store, Habitat for Humanity ReStore, or another local charity. Check with that organization ahead of time on what they currently accept and any drop-off requirements, since policies vary by charity and change over time.

## Condition matters

Most donation centers only accept items in usable, resalable condition. Confirm with the specific charity before booking if you're unsure whether a piece qualifies.

## Receipts

If you want a donation receipt for tax purposes, ask for one directly from the charity at drop-off — BoxHauls doesn't issue donation receipts.

## Price example

A donation drop-off is priced the same as any single-item or load haul — see [pricing](/pricing/) for the formula, or [what a dump run costs](/pricing/cost-of-a-dump-run/) for a full-load donation run.

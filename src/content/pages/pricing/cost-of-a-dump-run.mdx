A dump run costs [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile, plus the landfill or transfer-station gate fee — passed through at cost.

## The formula

[[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile, plus whatever the landfill or transfer station charges at the gate for your load.

## How the gate fee works

The disposal fee is passed through exactly as charged — BoxHauls doesn't mark it up. See [dump fees in [[METRO]]](/guides/dump-fees-in-fresno/) for current rates at local transfer stations.

## Load size

Book by how much of the truck bed your load fills:

- **Quarter load** — a few bags or small items.
- **Half load** — several bags, small furniture, or yard waste.
- **Full load** — a packed bed, like a garage or yard cleanout.

Pick the closest match so the driver brings the right tier.

## What's not accepted

Hazardous materials — paint, chemicals, batteries, tires, and similar items — generally aren't accepted at the landfill and won't be hauled. See [dump fees in [[METRO]]](/guides/dump-fees-in-fresno/) for what local transfer stations specifically take.

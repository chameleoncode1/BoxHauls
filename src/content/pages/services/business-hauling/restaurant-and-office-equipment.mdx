BoxHauls moves restaurant and office equipment — commercial fridges, desks, and gear — with after-hours scheduling when it's needed.

## Commercial equipment

Commercial refrigerators, prep tables, desks, and office furniture are common loads for this kind of move, typically sized to [[TIER_3]].

## After-hours scheduling

Restaurants and offices often need equipment moved outside business hours. Ask about after-hours or overnight availability when you book — it depends on driver coverage at that time.

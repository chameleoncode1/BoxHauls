BoxHauls picks up furniture you bought at a thrift store, Habitat for Humanity ReStore, or consignment shop — same day, no truck of your own required.

## How it works

Buy the piece, then book a BoxHauls pickup from that store's address. The driver picks up during the store's hold window and brings it straight to you.

## Pickup windows

Most thrift and consignment stores hold a purchased item for a limited window — often a day or two, sometimes just hours. Confirm the store's hold policy when you buy, and book your pickup within it.

## Condition notes

Secondhand furniture sometimes needs a wipe-down or has visible wear. Let the driver know if a piece should be wrapped separately in the truck bed so it doesn't transfer dust or debris to other items.

## Which tier fits

Most single thrift-store pieces — a dresser, a bookshelf, a side table — fit [[TIER_1]] or [[TIER_2]]. See [truck sizes](/pricing/truck-sizes/) for exact fit.

## Price example

A single piece picked up 10 miles from a Fresno thrift store runs about $48 before any helper fee — see [pricing](/pricing/) for the full formula.

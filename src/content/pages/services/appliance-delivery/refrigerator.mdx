BoxHauls delivers refrigerators upright, always — with the price shown before you book.

## Upright transport only

Refrigerators travel upright, never on their side, to avoid sending compressor oil into the cooling lines and damaging the unit.

## Defrost 24 hours ahead

Unplug and defrost at least 24 hours before pickup so it isn't dripping or running during transport.

## Door removal for tight doorways

If the refrigerator won't clear a doorway with the doors on, the driver can remove them and reattach at the drop-off.

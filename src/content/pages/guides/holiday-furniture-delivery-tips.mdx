Getting furniture delivered before guests arrive for the holidays comes down to booking early — pricing doesn't change, but availability tightens as the date gets closer.

## Book as early as possible

Once you know your date, book as soon as you can. Same-day availability depends on driver coverage in [[METRO]] at the time you book, and that coverage gets tighter as a busy date approaches — the same pattern as any other high-demand day.

## No holiday surcharge

BoxHauls prices the same formula year-round — [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile — with no seasonal surge pricing.

## Store delivery windows vs. same-day pickup

If a store's own delivery window won't get furniture there in time, picking it up yourself via BoxHauls the same day you buy it is often faster — see [store pickup](/services/furniture-delivery/store-pickup/) for how that works.

## Have a backup plan

For a hard deadline like guests arriving, build in a buffer day if you can — even with early booking, same-day availability isn't guaranteed on the busiest dates.

Getting lumber home from Home Depot without your own truck works the same way as any store pickup — buy it, then have a BoxHauls driver load it at the lumber yard.

## Pickup at the lumber yard

Complete your purchase, then meet the driver at the lumber pickup area — the same spot a personal truck would load from.

## Overhang and the red flag

Lumber longer than the truck bed overhangs the tailgate. When it does, a red flag (or reflective marker after dark) goes on the overhanging end.

## How much fits

A standard load of dimensional lumber or a stack of sheet goods fits [[TIER_2]] — mention your material list when you book so the driver plans the load and brings the right tier.

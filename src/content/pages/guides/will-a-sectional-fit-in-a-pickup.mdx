A sectional fits a pickup bed one section at a time — it separates for transport and reassembles at the drop-off.

[PHOTO: sectional sofa sections loaded separately in a pickup bed]

## Section dimensions

| Section | L × D × H (in) | Typical weight |
|---|---|---|
| Corner/wedge piece | 65 × 65 × 35 | 100-150 lb |
| Armless section | 35-40 × 35 × 35 | 80-120 lb |
| Chaise section | 65 × 40 × 35 | 100-150 lb |
| Full sectional (3-4 pieces) | varies | 350-550 lb total |

## One trip or two

Most sectionals — 3 to 4 sections — fit in one trip in [[TIER_3]]. Larger 5+ piece sectionals may need careful stacking or a second trip; mention your exact piece count when you book.

## What to prep

Disconnect any sections that clip together, remove loose cushions, and note which section goes where for reassembly.

For a studio apartment, a truck and driver is usually cheaper than hiring movers — the load is small enough to fit one or two per-trip hauls instead of paying an hourly crew minimum.

## The studio-sized math

A typical studio — bed, dresser, small couch or table, boxes — fits [[TIER_2]] in one or two trips. That's [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile, per trip, versus a moving crew's hourly rate with a multi-hour minimum regardless of how small your load is.

## What movers add

A full moving crew brings more hands for heavier lifting and can pack boxes for you — useful for a larger load, less necessary for a studio's worth of stuff.

## What a truck and driver is built for

One or two people's worth of furniture and boxes, loaded, driven, and placed — see [studio apartment move](/services/small-moves/studio-apartment-move/) for what's included.

## Bottom line

For a studio, compare your expected trip count against an hourly moving quote — see [cost to move a studio apartment](/pricing/cost-to-move-a-studio-apartment/) for a worked price example.

BoxHauls delivers furniture and large items with a truck and driver — from a store, a Marketplace seller, or your own move — with the price shown before you book.

## Who uses this

- **Store buyers** picking up a couch, mattress, or dining set the same day instead of waiting on a store's delivery window.
- **Marketplace buyers** who found a couch on Facebook Marketplace or OfferUp but don't own a truck to get it home.
- **Single-item moves** — one dresser, one bookshelf, no full move needed.

## What's included

The driver loads your item, secures it for transport, drives to the drop-off, and carries it inside to the first room — not just curbside. Cushions, drawers, and loose parts get wrapped and protected for open-bed transport.

## Delivery windows

Book for now or schedule ahead. Same-day availability depends on driver coverage in [[METRO]] at the time you book.

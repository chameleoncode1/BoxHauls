Found it on Craigslist? Send the driver the listing link and the seller's number, and they handle the pickup.

## How it works

Craigslist listings often route through a relay email address rather than showing a phone number directly — use that to reach the seller, confirm the item and a meeting time, and get a direct number before you book.

## Meeting-point rules

Meet in a public, well-lit location where possible — Craigslist transactions are typically more anonymous than other platforms, so this matters even more here.

## What to send the driver

The listing link and the seller's phone number, once you have it.

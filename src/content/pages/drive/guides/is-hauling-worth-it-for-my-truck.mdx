Whether hauling is worth it for your specific truck comes down to fuel cost and wear per mile versus what a haul actually pays — there's no single answer for every vehicle.

## The real cost per mile

Fuel is the most obvious cost, but wear — tires, brakes, general depreciation — adds up too, even though it's less visible on a day-to-day basis. Neither is reimbursed separately by BoxHauls; they come out of your [[DRIVER_SHARE]]% share of the fare.

## Fuel economy varies by truck class

Heavier-duty trucks generally get worse fuel economy than smaller ones, which matters more the more hauling miles you rack up — worth factoring in if you're deciding what to drive for hauls.

## Weighing a specific haul

Before accepting, weigh the fare shown against the round-trip distance and your truck's real cost per mile — a short, nearby haul is usually a clearer win than a long one where fuel eats a bigger share of the payout.

## Gear that reduces wear on you, if not the truck

A dolly and ramp won't change your fuel cost, but they cut down on strain and time per haul — see [driver equipment](/drive/equipment/) for the full kit.

BoxHauls runs jobsite material pickups — from the supply house straight to your site, same day when driver coverage allows.

## Supply-house pickup

Lumber, fixtures, and building material picked up from a supply house and delivered directly to the jobsite — no waiting on the supplier's own delivery schedule.

## Overhang and red flags

Lumber or material longer than the truck bed overhangs the tailgate. When it does, a red flag (or reflective marker after dark) goes on the overhanging end — standard practice for oversized loads.

## Same-day runs

Same-day material runs depend on driver coverage in [[METRO]] at the time you book — book as early in the day as possible.

## Price example

A jobsite material run is priced the same as any per-trip haul — see [cost to deliver plywood and lumber](/pricing/cost-to-deliver-plywood-and-lumber/) for a worked example.

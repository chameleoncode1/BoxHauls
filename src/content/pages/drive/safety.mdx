Practical safety guidance for hauling — lifting, meeting points, weather, and when to walk away.

## Lifting

Use a dolly for anything heavy rather than lifting alone — see [equipment every hauling driver should carry](/drive/equipment/). Bend at the knees, not the back, and ask about a helper add-on if a job looks like a two-person load.

## Meeting-point safety

For Marketplace pickups especially, a public, well-lit meeting point is safer than an isolated address. Trust your judgment — if something feels off, you can cancel and report it.

## Summer heat

[[METRO]] regularly hits 100°F or higher from June through September. Stay hydrated, take breaks in shade when you can, and be cautious with appliances or electronics that shouldn't sit in direct heat too long between pickup and drop-off.

## What to refuse

You're never required to complete a haul that puts you at risk. Refuse — and report — a load that exceeds your truck's safe capacity, a meeting point that feels unsafe, or an item that doesn't match what was booked.

## Mattress dimensions

| Size | Dimensions | Typical weight |
|---|---|---|
| Twin | 38 × 75 in | 50–80 lb |
| Full | 54 × 75 in | 70–100 lb |
| Queen | 60 × 80 in | 90–130 lb |
| King | 76 × 80 in | 120–150 lb |

Twin and full mattresses fit [[TIER_1]]; queen and king typically need [[TIER_2]]. See [truck sizes](/pricing/truck-sizes/) for exact fit.

## Do you need a helper?

Queen and king mattresses are awkward for one person through doorways and stairs. Add a helper if either end of the haul has stairs — see [when you need a second helper](/guides/when-you-need-a-second-helper/).

## What to prep

A mattress bag is recommended, especially for open-bed transport, to keep it clean and dry. Clear a path and measure doorways before pickup.

## Overhang and the red flag

A kayak longer than the truck bed overhangs the tailgate. When it does, a red flag (or reflective marker after dark) goes on the overhanging end — standard practice for any load that extends past the bed.

## Tier

A single kayak usually fits [[TIER_1]]. Multiple kayaks or a kayak with paddles, life vests, and a trailer's worth of gear may need [[TIER_2]].

## What to prep

Have straps or a rack ready if you have one, and remove loose gear from inside the kayak before pickup.

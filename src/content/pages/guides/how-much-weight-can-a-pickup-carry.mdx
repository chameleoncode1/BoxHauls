A pickup's payload capacity — how much it can safely carry — varies by truck, typically in the 1,500-3,000 lb range for common models.

[PHOTO: loaded pickup truck bed with visible suspension sag under weight]

## Payload vs. towing

Payload is what the truck carries in the bed and cabin, including passengers and everything loaded on board. Towing capacity is a separate rating for trailers — the two aren't the same number.

## What overloading looks like

A truck carrying more than its rated payload handles and brakes worse, since the suspension and tires are working beyond their design limit. Visible signs include the rear end sagging noticeably lower than normal.

## How BoxHauls sizes the load

Drivers work within their specific truck's rated payload — BoxHauls matches your item's approximate weight to a tier built to carry it. See [truck sizes](/pricing/truck-sizes/) for what each tier is rated to handle.

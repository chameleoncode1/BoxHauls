Apply to offer BoxHauls at your store — no fee, no reciprocal-link requirements, just a delivery option for customers who need one.

## How to apply

Email [[EMAIL]] or call [[PHONE]] with your store name, location, and what kind of business you run.

## What happens next

BoxHauls follows up to confirm your business type and set up whichever checkout flow fits your store — a QR code at the register, a link on receipts or order confirmations, or staff booking it directly for customers.

## No cost

There's no fee to become a partner. Customers pay for their own delivery at the standard [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile rate — your store isn't billed for offering it.

See [partners](/partners/) for how it works by business type.

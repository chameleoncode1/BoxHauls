Motorcycle hauls in [[METRO]] use the standard formula — but only a driver equipped with a ramp and proper tie-downs can take the job.

## The formula

[[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile, when an equipped driver is available.

| Distance | Base price | With a helper (+ [[HELPER_FEE]]) |
|---|---|---|
| 3 miles | $26.30 | $43.30 |
| 10 miles | $48.00 | $65.00 |
| 25 miles | $94.50 | $111.50 |

[[HELPER_AVAILABILITY_NOTE]]

## Equipment requirement

Hauling a motorcycle safely requires a loading ramp and secure tie-down points — not every BoxHauls driver carries this equipment. If no equipped driver is available for your haul, BoxHauls will let you know so you can find a specialist motorcycle transporter instead.

## Tier

[[TIER_3]] — a motorcycle needs ramp loading and tie-down capacity that only a heavier-duty setup provides.

## What to prep

Confirm the bike is in neutral with the kickstand accessible, and have the title or registration on hand if requested.

[Book this haul](/book/?item=motorcycle&tier=tier-3) — the price shows before you confirm, and BoxHauls will let you know if an equipped driver isn't available.

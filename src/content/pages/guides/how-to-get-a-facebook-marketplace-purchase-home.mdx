Getting a Facebook Marketplace purchase home safely starts with a public meeting spot and a close look at the item before you pay.

## Before you go

Message the seller to confirm the item is still available and get exact pickup details. Ask for extra photos if anything in the listing is unclear.

## Meeting the seller

Meet in a public, well-lit location where the listing allows it. Bring exact payment or use a payment app agreed on in advance.

## Inspecting the item

Check the item matches the listing before you pay — see [how to inspect used furniture before buying](/guides/how-to-inspect-used-furniture-before-buying/) for what to look for.

## Getting it home

If you don't have a vehicle for the item, or don't want to make the trip yourself, book a [Facebook Marketplace pickup](/services/furniture-delivery/marketplace-pickup/facebook-marketplace/) — send the driver the listing link and the seller's contact info, and they handle the pickup.

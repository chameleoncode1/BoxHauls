BoxHauls delivers water heaters — kept upright the entire trip, with installation left to a licensed plumber.

## Tank sizes

Standard residential tank sizes are handled the same way — the driver keeps the tank upright throughout loading, transport, and unloading.

## Upright, always

Laying a water heater on its side risks damaging the internal components. It travels standing up for the entire haul, no exceptions.

## Installation

The driver delivers and places the unit, but connecting gas, electric, or water lines is a licensed plumber's job — coordinate installation separately.

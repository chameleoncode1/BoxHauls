Most washer and dryer moves in [[METRO]] cost between $26 and $95, depending on distance — [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile, plus a helper fee since moving two appliances is a two-person job.

## The formula

[[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile. A helper is effectively required for this haul, since it's a two-person job.

| Distance | Base price | With a helper (+ [[HELPER_FEE]]) |
|---|---|---|

## Washer and dryer specs

Most units weigh 150–250 lb each. Standard washer and dryer pairs fit together in [[TIER_2]]. See [truck sizes](/pricing/truck-sizes/) for exact fit.

## Do you need a helper?

Yes — moving two appliances is always a two-person job, so a helper is effectively required rather than optional.

## What to prep

Install the shipping bolts that lock the drum in place, if you have them — this protects the washer from damage during transport. Disconnect hoses and cords, and clear a path at both ends.

A one-bedroom apartment typically moves in two trips — priced per trip, not as one bundled job.

## Two trips typical

Expect one trip for bedroom furniture (bed, dresser, nightstands) and a second for the living room, kitchen boxes, and anything else. Each trip is priced separately.

## When to add a second truck

If you're on a tight timeline — a lease turnover with a hard move-out deadline, for example — booking a second truck to run in parallel is faster than one truck making two trips back to back.

## Price example

Two trips at 10 miles each run about $96 total before any helper fee — see [pricing](/pricing/) for the full formula and other distances.

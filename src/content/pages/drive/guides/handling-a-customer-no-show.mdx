If a customer doesn't show up at pickup, message them through the app, wait a reasonable amount of time, and end the haul through the app if there's still no response.

## Message first

Reach out to the customer through the app as soon as you arrive if they're not there — this is the fastest way to reach them and keeps a record of the attempt.

## Wait a reasonable amount of time

Give the customer a reasonable window to respond or arrive before deciding the haul can't happen — don't leave immediately, but don't wait indefinitely either.

## Document and end the haul

If there's still no response after a reasonable wait, end the haul through the app rather than just leaving. This keeps a clear record of what happened.

## For Marketplace pickups specifically

A no-show is more common when picking up from a Marketplace seller rather than the customer themselves — the same wait-and-document approach applies either way.

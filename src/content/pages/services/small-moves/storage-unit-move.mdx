BoxHauls loads and hauls a storage unit's contents — one trip for smaller units, two for larger ones.

## Unit sizes to loads

A 5x5 or 5x10 unit usually fits one [[TIER_3]] load. A 10x10 typically needs two trips.

## Gate codes and hours

Have your facility's gate code and unit number ready for the driver, and check the facility's access hours before booking — most self-storage facilities restrict access outside certain hours.

## Price example

A one-trip storage unit move at 10 miles runs about $48 before any helper fee — see [pricing](/pricing/) for the full formula and larger-unit examples.

BoxHauls hauls junk by the truckload — priced by load size, with the dump fee passed through at cost.

## Per-load pricing

Book by how much of the truck bed your load fills — quarter, half, or full. See [what a dump run costs](/pricing/cost-of-a-dump-run/) for the formula and how the gate fee is added.

## What's accepted

General household junk, furniture, appliances (see [appliance haul-away](/services/appliance-delivery/haul-away/) for refrigerant rules), and yard waste.

## What's not accepted

Hazardous materials — paint, chemicals, batteries, tires, and similar items — generally aren't accepted at the landfill and won't be hauled.

## How dump fees pass through

The landfill or transfer-station gate fee is charged exactly as billed at the gate — BoxHauls doesn't mark it up.

## Donation-first

Usable furniture and household items are often better donated than landfilled — ask about donation-first options when you book.

## Local transfer stations

[[METRO]]'s two main options are American Avenue Disposal Site and the Cedar Avenue Recycling & Transfer Station (CARTS) — see [dump fees in [[METRO]]](/guides/dump-fees-in-fresno/) for current hours and fees.

A refrigerator fits a pickup bed upright — never laid down, since that risks damaging the cooling system.

[PHOTO: refrigerator strapped upright in a pickup bed]

## Dimensions

| Refrigerator type | L × D × H (in) | Typical weight |
|---|---|---|
| Top-freezer | 30 × 30 × 66 | 200-300 lb |
| Side-by-side | 36 × 30 × 70 | 250-350 lb |
| French door | 36 × 30 × 70 | 280-380 lb |

## Why it stays upright

Laying a refrigerator on its side can let compressor oil shift into the cooling lines, which can damage the compressor. It travels standing up for the whole trip, and needs to rest upright for a period after any tilting before being plugged in.

## Fit and tie-down

Most refrigerators are under 36 in wide, fitting within a standard bed's width. Height often means the tailgate stays down and the unit rides secured with straps against the cab.

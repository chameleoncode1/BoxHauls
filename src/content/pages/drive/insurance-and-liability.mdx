## What personal auto policies typically exclude

Most personal auto insurance policies exclude commercial or business use of your vehicle — and gig hauling counts as business use in most states. This is a standard exclusion across the industry, not specific to BoxHauls, so check your policy's language before you start.

## Commercial add-on options

Some insurers offer a rideshare or delivery endorsement that extends personal coverage to gig work for a relatively small added premium. Not every insurer offers one, and terms vary — ask directly whether their endorsement covers hauling large items, not just rideshare driving.

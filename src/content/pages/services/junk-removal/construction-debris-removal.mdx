BoxHauls hauls construction debris — drywall, tile, and similar material — by the load, with weight limits in mind.

## What's accepted

Drywall, tile, flooring scraps, and similar construction debris. Priced the same per-load rate as other junk removal.

## Weight vs. volume

Dense material like tile, concrete, and drywall can hit the truck's payload limit well before the bed looks full — the driver will let you know if a load has reached capacity, since weight matters more than volume for this kind of debris.

## Contractor accounts

Running regular jobsite debris hauls? See [business hauling](/services/business-hauling/) for recurring-pickup and account options built for contractors and suppliers.

## Price example

A load of construction debris at 10 miles runs a per-load rate plus the gate fee, passed through at cost — see [what a dump run costs](/pricing/cost-of-a-dump-run/) for the formula.

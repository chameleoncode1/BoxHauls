BoxHauls picks up from the Home Depot Pro Desk or regular checkout and delivers — lumber, appliances, and anything too big for your own car.

## Pro Desk pickup

Contractor and bulk orders are typically staged at the Pro Desk rather than regular checkout — mention this when you book if that's where your order is.

## Lumber overhang

Long items like plywood and lumber that extend past the truck bed are handled with a red flag and proper securement, per standard road safety practice. See [cost to deliver plywood and lumber](/pricing/cost-to-deliver-plywood-and-lumber/).

## Appliances

Home Depot sells major appliances too — see [appliance delivery](/services/appliance-delivery/) for prep and handling notes.

## Cheaper than renting their truck

For a single one-way trip, BoxHauls is often cheaper than Home Depot's own truck rental once you account for the rental fee, fuel, and your own time driving and returning it.

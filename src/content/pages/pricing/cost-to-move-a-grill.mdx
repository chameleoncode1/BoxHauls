## Propane tank rules

The propane tank rides separately from the grill, not together in an enclosed space — a standard safety practice for transporting any propane cylinder.

## Weight

Most grills weigh 100-200 lb and fit [[TIER_1]] or [[TIER_2]].

## What to prep

Disconnect and separate the propane tank, and clean out any ash or grease before pickup.

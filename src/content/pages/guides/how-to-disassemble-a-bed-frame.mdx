Disassembling a bed frame comes down to five pieces in order: headboard and footboard first, then the side rails, then the center support and slats.

[PHOTO: disassembled bed frame parts laid out with hardware bagged separately]

## Clear the bed

Remove the mattress, box spring, and bedding before starting — you need a clear path to the frame itself.

## Headboard and footboard first

Unbolt the headboard and footboard from the side rails — most frames use a few bolts or a hook-and-slot connection that lifts apart once unbolted.

## Side rails

Unbolt or unhook the side rails from each other and from any center support beam.

## Center support and slats

Remove the center support beam and any slats — usually the simplest pieces, often just resting in place rather than bolted.

## Keep hardware together

Bag all bolts, screws, and brackets together and tape the bag to one of the larger frame pieces so nothing gets lost between disassembly and reassembly.

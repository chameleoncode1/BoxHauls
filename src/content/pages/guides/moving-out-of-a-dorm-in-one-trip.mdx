A dorm room almost always fits in one truck trip — the key to making it fast is having everything boxed and staged before the driver arrives.

## What fits in one trip

Mini-fridge, futon, boxes, a desk chair, and typical dorm belongings fit [[TIER_1]] or [[TIER_2]] in a single load.

## Stage before the driver arrives

Have everything boxed, labeled, and staged near the door or curb ahead of time — packing on the spot while the driver waits slows down move-out day for everyone in line behind you.

## Booking around move-out week

Move-out days get busy campus-wide, and same-day availability tightens as a result. Book as early as you know your date.

BoxHauls picks up from estate sales and auctions — one item or several, with pieces wrapped and secured for the drive.

## Pickup timing

Estate sales and auctions often require pickup within a tight window, sometimes the next day. Book your BoxHauls haul as soon as you know your pickup time so you don't miss it.

## Multiple items in one trip

Bought more than one piece? A single trip can carry everything that fits the tier you book — mention your full list when you book so the driver brings the right size truck.

## Fragile pieces

China cabinets, mirrors, glass-top tables, and similar fragile items get wrapped and secured separately for open-bed transport. Flag anything fragile when you book.

## Which tier fits

A few smaller pieces fit [[TIER_1]] or [[TIER_2]]; a larger estate-sale haul — furniture plus boxes of smaller items — may need [[TIER_3]]. See [truck sizes](/pricing/truck-sizes/) for exact fit.

## Price example

A multi-piece estate-sale pickup at 10 miles runs about $48 in [[TIER_2]] before any helper fee — see [pricing](/pricing/) for the full formula.

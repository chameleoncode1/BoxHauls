A mattress-in-a-box is far easier to transport than a traditional mattress — compressed and boxed, it's smaller, lighter to maneuver, and doesn't need the same open-bed protection.

## Size and handling

A compressed, boxed mattress is a fraction of the size of the same mattress uncompressed — it fits in more vehicles and is easier for one person to handle, though the box itself is still heavy and shouldn't be dropped or bent sharply.

## Traditional delivery

A traditional, already-expanded mattress needs to travel flat or upright depending on space, ideally bagged for open-bed transport — see [how to move a mattress without getting it dirty](/guides/how-to-move-a-mattress-without-getting-it-dirty/).

## Which one needs a truck more

Ironically, a boxed mattress is the one that sometimes fits in a car — it's the already-unboxed, expanded mattress that most often needs a truck.

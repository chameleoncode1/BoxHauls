Every BoxHauls fee beyond the base fare and per-mile rate is listed below, with what triggers it.

| Fee | Trigger | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Heavy item | Item exceeds standard weight for its tier | [[HEAVY_FEE]] |
| Helper | You add a second person for stairs or a heavy item | [[HELPER_FEE]] |
| Stairs | Load or unload involves stairs | [[STAIRS_FEE]] |
| Wait time | Driver waits beyond the included grace period | [[WAIT_TIME_FEE]] |
| Late cancellation | Cancel after the free-cancellation window | [[CANCELLATION_FEE]] |

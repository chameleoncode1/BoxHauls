## Stove specs

Most stoves and ranges weigh 150-250 lb and fit [[TIER_2]].

## Gas disconnect

A gas stove needs to be disconnected by a licensed professional before pickup — this isn't something the driver does. Electric ranges just need to be unplugged.

## Do you need a helper?

Often, yes — most stoves are heavy enough that two people make loading and unloading much easier, especially through doorways.

## What to prep

Have gas lines professionally disconnected ahead of time, and clear the space around the stove before the driver arrives.

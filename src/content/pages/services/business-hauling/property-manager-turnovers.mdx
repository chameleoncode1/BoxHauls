BoxHauls clears left-behind furniture and junk between tenants — priced per load, built for tight turnover windows.

## Left-behind furniture

Tenants leaving furniture, mattresses, or general junk behind is common. Book a haul to clear it before the next move-in — see [junk removal](/services/junk-removal/) for per-load pricing.

## 48-hour turns

A tight turnover window works the same as any other booking — availability depends on driver coverage at the time you book, so book as soon as you know the unit needs clearing.

## Recurring accounts

Managing multiple units or properties? See [business hauling](/services/business-hauling/) for recurring-account and invoicing options.

## Price example

A unit clear-out is priced per load — see [what a dump run costs](/pricing/cost-of-a-dump-run/) for the formula.

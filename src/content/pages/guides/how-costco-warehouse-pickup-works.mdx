Costco warehouse pickup for big items works the same for a BoxHauls driver as it would for you — buy it, then have it loaded at the pickup area.

## How pickup at the warehouse works

Complete your purchase inside, then bring your receipt to the loading area (or curbside pickup zone, depending on the item) where the driver meets you.

## Booking around your purchase

Book your BoxHauls pickup once you know your item is ready — the driver arrives to load it the same way a personal vehicle would.

## Common Costco pickup items

Furniture, large appliances, patio sets, and bulky seasonal items are common reasons shoppers need a truck instead of a car.

See [Costco pickup](/services/furniture-delivery/store-pickup/costco/) for pricing and store details.

Home Depot's truck rental is a paid, time-based program, not a free loan — you're still doing the driving, loading, and unloading yourself.

## Pricing

The Load 'N Go program charges a flat starting rate for the first 75 minutes (roughly $19-$29 depending on vehicle), then $5 per additional 15 minutes up to a daily rate cap, with unlimited mileage included. It is not free with a purchase — that's a common assumption worth correcting before you plan around it.

## Requirements

You need to be 21 or older with a valid driver's license, and show proof of personal auto liability insurance. No commercial license is required.

## Insurance add-ons

Optional protection includes a Collision/Limited Damage Waiver, Supplemental Liability Insurance for third-party claims, and goods-in-transit insurance up to $15,000, plus a damage protection plan with a $500 limit for non-collision damage.

## Where Home Depot rental wins

If you're already at the store buying materials and just need to get them home quickly, the time-based rate can be cheap for a short trip — and unlimited mileage helps for longer drives.

## Where BoxHauls wins

No driving a rental truck yourself, and no time pressure to return it within a specific window — the driver handles loading, the drive, and unloading as part of one flat price.

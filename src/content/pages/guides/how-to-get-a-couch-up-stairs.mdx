Getting a couch up stairs comes down to measuring first, tilting it on its side, and having a second person on the other end.

[PHOTO: two people carrying a couch tilted on its side up a stairwell]

## Measure the stairwell

Measure the stairwell's width and any turns or landings before you start, including diagonal clearance at corners.

## Remove what you can

Take off legs, cushions, and any detachable parts to reduce bulk and weight.

## Tilt on its side

Tilting the couch onto its side reduces its effective width through tight turns and doorways.

## Lead with the right end

Lead with the heavier or bulkier end going up the stairs, so the lighter end is easier to maneuver around turns.

## Use a second person

A couch is heavy and awkward enough that two people is the practical minimum for stairs — add a helper when you book. [[HELPER_AVAILABILITY_NOTE]]

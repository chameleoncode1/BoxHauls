Most couch moves in [[METRO]] cost between $26 and $95, depending on distance — [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile.

## The formula

[[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile. A couch itself doesn't add a fee unless it's unusually heavy; adding a helper does.

| Distance | Base price | With a helper (+ [[HELPER_FEE]]) |
|---|---|---|
| 3 miles | $26.30 | $43.30 |
| 10 miles | $48.00 | $65.00 |
| 25 miles | $94.50 | $111.50 |

[[HELPER_AVAILABILITY_NOTE]]

## Couch dimensions

Most couches are 72–96 in long and 100–200 lb. That fits [[TIER_2]], BoxHauls's standard truck tier — see [truck sizes](/pricing/truck-sizes/) for what fits where.

## Do you need a helper?

One person can usually handle a couch move on flat ground with no stairs. Add a helper for stairs, a tight doorway, or a heavier sectional-style couch — see [when you need a second helper](/guides/when-you-need-a-second-helper/).

## What to prep

Remove and secure loose cushions, measure doorways at both the pickup and drop-off, and clear a path for the driver. They handle wrapping and securing the couch itself.

Hot tub removal and disposal is straightforward. Relocating one to a new spot is a bigger job — here's what each takes.

## Removal and disposal

Yes — BoxHauls hauls away an old hot tub for disposal, similar to any other junk removal job. See [junk removal](/services/junk-removal/) for per-load pricing.

## Relocation

Moving a hot tub to a new spot (not disposal) needs [[TIER_3]] and two helpers, given the weight and awkward shape. Pricing: [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile, plus two helper fees (2 x [[HELPER_FEE]]).

[[HELPER_AVAILABILITY_NOTE]] With two helpers needed, availability matters even more — confirm before you count on same-day relocation.

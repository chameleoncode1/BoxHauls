For a single item or a small load, hiring a full moving crew usually costs more than it needs to — most local movers bill hourly with a multi-hour minimum, regardless of how small the job is.

## How movers typically price

Local moving companies commonly charge $80-$165/hour for a 2-person crew and truck nationally (more in expensive metros), with a 2-4 hour minimum that applies even to a quick, small job. A 3-person crew often runs $130-$170/hour; 4 people, $180-$210/hour.

## Where movers win

A large multi-room household move genuinely benefits from a full crew — more hands for heavy furniture, and some companies offer packing services BoxHauls doesn't.

## Where BoxHauls wins

Anything from a single item up through a studio or one-bedroom's worth of furniture — the hourly-minimum math on a small job with a moving company rarely favors the mover. See [truck and driver hourly vs. per-trip pricing](/pricing/truck-and-driver-hourly-vs-per-trip/) for the detailed math.

## Verdict

For one item to a small apartment, BoxHauls avoids paying for a crew-hour minimum you don't need — see [pricing](/pricing/). For a full household move with heavy furniture and multiple rooms, a moving crew may be the better fit.

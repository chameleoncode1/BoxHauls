Tipping a truck delivery driver is optional — there's no required amount, and the price you see before booking never includes one.

## Is it expected?

No — the price shown before you book is the full cost. A tip is a bonus for good service, not a hidden expectation.

## What people typically consider

Some riders tip a flat amount for a straightforward haul, and more for a harder job — stairs, a heavy item, bad weather. There's no fixed rule; it's entirely your call based on the service you received.

## How to tip

Tip through the app after the haul completes, or hand cash directly to the driver if you prefer.

## Where it goes

A tip goes entirely to the driver — see [how BoxHauls works](/how-it-works/) for how the rest of the price breaks down.

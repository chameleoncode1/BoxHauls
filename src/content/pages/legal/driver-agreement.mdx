**[[LEGAL_REVIEW_STATUS]]**

This page lists the sections a BoxHauls Driver Agreement will cover. The actual binding agreement is drafted and reviewed by legal counsel — including California worker-classification review — before this page is finalized. Nothing below is a legal commitment yet.

## Independent contractor status

That drivers operate as independent contractors, not employees, and what that means for scheduling, taxes, and benefits.

## Vehicle and equipment requirements

The truck condition, age, and equipment standards drivers must meet.

## Background checks and ongoing standards

The vetting process before activation and what keeps a driver in good standing.

## Payment terms

How and when drivers are paid, and how the fare split is calculated.

## Insurance requirements

The insurance a driver must carry, and how it relates to coverage BoxHauls provides.

## Conduct and safety standards

Expected conduct during hauls, including safety and customer-property handling.

## Termination

How either party may end the relationship.

## Indemnification and liability

How liability is allocated between BoxHauls and the driver.

## Dispute resolution

How disputes between BoxHauls and a driver are resolved.

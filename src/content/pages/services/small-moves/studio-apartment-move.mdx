A studio apartment usually moves in one truck load — occasionally two if you have a lot of furniture or boxes.

## Trips

Most studios fit in one [[TIER_3]] load: a bed, a dresser, a couch or loveseat, and boxes. If you have more than that, book a second trip.

## Box counts

Expect room for roughly 10-15 standard boxes alongside your furniture in one load — fewer if you're bringing a couch or sectional too.

## Helper

Moving solo with a bed frame, dresser, or stairs involved? Add a helper — see [when you need a second helper](/guides/when-you-need-a-second-helper/).

## Price example

A one-trip studio move at 10 miles runs about $48 before any helper fee — see [pricing](/pricing/) for the full formula.

## Dimensions

Peloton-style bikes and rowers fit [[TIER_1]] or [[TIER_2]].

## Screen protection

Peloton-style bikes have a large touchscreen that needs to be wrapped and protected separately from the frame during transport.

## Do you need a helper?

Often, yes — these bikes are heavier and more awkward to carry than a standard stationary bike, especially through doorways.

## What to prep

Wrap the screen, secure any loose pedals or accessories, and clear a path to the door.

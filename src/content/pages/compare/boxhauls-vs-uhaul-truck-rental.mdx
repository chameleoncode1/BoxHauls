Renting a U-Haul means you drive, load, and unload it yourself — BoxHauls sends someone to do that part.

## The cost comparison

U-Haul's local in-town pickup truck rate commonly starts around $19.95/day plus roughly $0.79-$1.29 per mile, depending on location and day. That's often cheaper than a single BoxHauls trip on paper — but it doesn't include your time loading and unloading, fuel, or the deposit some renters need to leave. A BoxHauls trip is [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]]/mile, with a driver handling the loading and unloading.

## License and requirements

Only a standard driver's license is needed to rent a U-Haul pickup truck, van, or small box truck — no commercial license required for these categories. No deposit is required if paying by card; cash renters typically leave a deposit.

## Insurance

U-Haul doesn't automatically include damage or goods-in-transit protection — it's sold separately as Safemove or Safemove Plus. Without purchasing one, you're relying on your own auto insurance, which may or may not cover a rental truck.

## Where U-Haul wins

If you already have people to help load and unload, and don't mind driving, the sticker price alone can be lower — especially for a longer rental where you use the truck multiple times.

## Where BoxHauls wins

No loading, no driving, no separate insurance decision to make, and a single price that includes the labor — worth it the moment your own time or a lack of helping hands enters the equation.

## Verdict

If you value your time and don't want to load, drive, and unload yourself, BoxHauls is the simpler option — see [pricing](/pricing/) for a direct price.

## What BoxHauls is

An on-demand marketplace that sends a local pickup-truck owner to move one or a few large items between two addresses, priced per trip, with the price shown before booking.

## What BoxHauls is not

BoxHauls is not long-haul trucking or LTL shipping — it's local, same-day hauls. It's not long-distance moving — it's one truck, one trip, within [[METRO]]. It's not dumpster rental — a driver hauls your items away, rather than leaving a bin. And it's not rideshare — BoxHauls moves things, not people.

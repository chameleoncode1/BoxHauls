BoxHauls handles downsizing moves — a partial load of what's actually going to the new place, with donation drop-off available on the same trip.

## Partial loads

Downsizing rarely means moving everything — book just the pieces going to the new address. A partial load often fits [[TIER_1]] or [[TIER_2]].

## Donation drop-offs

Furniture and household items not going to the new place can go straight to donation on the same trip — ask about a donation stop when you book. See [furniture donation drop-off](/services/junk-removal/furniture-donation-pickup/) for how that works.

## Pace

Downsizing moves often involve decisions made on the spot about what to keep. Let the driver know ahead of time if you'd like a bit of extra time during loading — drivers work at a reasonable, unhurried pace.

## Price example

A partial downsizing load at 10 miles runs about $48 in [[TIER_1]] before any helper fee — see [pricing](/pricing/) for the full formula.

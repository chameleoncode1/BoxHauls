BoxHauls delivers washers and dryers together or separately — with two-person handling standard.

## Shipping bolts

If you have the original shipping bolts, install them before pickup — they lock the drum in place during transport and prevent internal damage. Ask the driver about options if you don't have them.

## Stacking kits

If your washer and dryer are stacked, a stacking kit secures them for the move — mention it when you book so the driver knows what to expect.

## Dryer vent note

The driver delivers and places the dryer, but connecting the vent is a job for a licensed installer, not part of the haul.

Add a second helper for stairs, heavy appliances, or a tight doorway — a single driver handles most flat, ground-level single-item hauls alone.

## When it's worth it

- **Stairs** — carrying anything heavier than a box up or down stairs is faster and safer with two people.
- **Heavy appliances** — refrigerators, washers, and similar items are awkward for one person even on flat ground.
- **Tight doorways** — maneuvering a couch or mattress through a narrow turn often needs a second set of hands.
- **Long carries** — a long distance from the truck to the door adds up fast for one person alone.

## When you probably don't need one

A single item on flat, ground-level access — a chair, a small dresser, boxes — is usually fine for one driver.

## The cost

A helper adds a flat [[HELPER_FEE]] fee to your haul. [[HELPER_AVAILABILITY_NOTE]]

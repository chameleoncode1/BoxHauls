BoxHauls offers last-mile delivery for retailers — a checkout delivery option without running your own fleet.

## Delivery at checkout

Retail partners can offer BoxHauls as a same-day or scheduled delivery option right at checkout, instead of quoting a delivery window days out or managing their own trucks.

## No fleet required

Retailers don't need to own or schedule delivery vehicles — BoxHauls handles the last-mile leg from store to customer.

## Setting up the partner program

Contact BoxHauls through [partners](/partners/) to set up a retail integration for your store.

## Price model

Retail last-mile delivery follows the same per-trip pricing model, with business-account invoicing available — see [business hauling](/services/business-hauling/) for account setup.

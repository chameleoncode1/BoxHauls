Wrapping furniture for an open truck bed means moving blankets for fabric, plastic wrap for extra weather protection, and separate padding for anything glass.

[PHOTO: furniture wrapped in moving blankets and secured in a pickup bed]

## Wrap fabric pieces in moving blankets

Cover upholstered furniture — couches, chairs, mattresses — with moving blankets to protect against dust, dirt, and moisture during the drive.

## Plastic-wrap exposed cushions

Add plastic wrap over cushions and fabric edges for extra protection, since open-bed transport means direct weather exposure.

## Protect corners and legs

Wrap sharp corners and exposed table or chair legs separately so they don't puncture or scratch other wrapped items nearby.

## Wrap glass and mirrors separately

Glass tabletops and mirrors need their own padding and should never rest directly against another hard surface.

## Secure the wrap itself

Tape or strap the wrapping in place so it doesn't unravel or blow off during the drive — a loose blanket does no good.

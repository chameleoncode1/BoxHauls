BoxHauls works in eight steps: enter your addresses, pick your item and truck tier, see the price, get matched with a driver, track and message them, watch them load and unload, pay, and rate the haul.

## Enter addresses, item, and tier

Start with your pickup and drop-off addresses. Pick what's moving and a [truck tier](/pricing/truck-sizes/) — [[TIER_1]], [[TIER_2]], or [[TIER_3]], depending on size and weight. The price appears once both addresses and an item are entered: [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile, plus any item or helper fees. See the full breakdown on [pricing](/pricing/).

## Matched with a driver

Once you book, a nearby driver accepts the haul. You can message them directly in the app — no phone number is shared until they accept.

## Payment hold

When you book, BoxHauls places a hold on your card for the full quoted price. It's captured once the haul is marked complete, so you're never charged before the truck shows up.

## Load, haul, unload

The driver loads your item, secures it for transport, drives to the drop-off, and unloads it. If you added a helper, they handle stairs and tight doorways with you.

## Cancellation window

Plans change — see the [cancellation and refund policy](/trust/cancellation-and-refunds/) for the free-cancellation window and late-cancel fee.

## Pay and rate

Once the haul is complete, the held payment is captured and you rate the driver in the app.

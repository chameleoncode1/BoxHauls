Buy it at the store, and a BoxHauls driver picks it up and brings it home the same day — no waiting on the store's own delivery window.

## How it works

Give the driver your order number and confirmation once you book. They head to the store's pickup lane or will-call counter, present ID, and load your item. You don't need to be there for pickup — just for delivery.

## Which stores

BoxHauls drivers pick up from Costco, Living Spaces, Home Depot, Lowe's, Ashley Furniture, Big Lots, Walmart, Target, and Mattress Firm locations in [[METRO]], among others.

- [IKEA](/services/furniture-delivery/store-pickup/ikea/)

## Same-day windows

Same-day pickup depends on driver availability and how quickly the store confirms your order is ready — check your order status before booking for the fastest turnaround.

The real cost of a truck rental is more than the daily rate — mileage, fuel, insurance, and your own time loading and driving all add up.

## What a rental actually costs

A rental quote usually covers just the base daily rate. On top of that: mileage charges beyond the included allowance, fuel (you return it full), and often an insurance add-on if you don't have coverage that extends to rented trucks. Then there's picking up and returning the truck itself, which takes real time.

## What hiring a truck and driver costs

BoxHauls prices per trip: [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile, with no separate fuel or insurance line — a 10-mile haul runs $48 total, with the driver doing the loading, driving, and unloading. See [pricing](/pricing/) for the full formula.

## When renting wins

For a full house's worth of items over a full day, a rental's per-day rate can beat paying for multiple trips.

## When hiring wins

For a single item or two, when you don't want to drive an unfamiliar truck, or when you need help loading and unloading, a truck and driver is usually less total hassle and often less total cost once rental add-ons are counted.

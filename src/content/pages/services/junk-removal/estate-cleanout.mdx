BoxHauls handles estate cleanouts — one load or several across multiple days, with donation-first options and a respectful pace.

## Multi-day cleanouts

A full estate cleanout is often more than one truck bed can hold. Book loads as you're ready, across as many days as the cleanout takes — there's no requirement to finish in one visit.

## Donation coordination

Usable furniture and household items are often better donated than sent to the landfill. Ask about a donation-first approach when you book, and see [furniture donation drop-off](/services/junk-removal/furniture-donation-pickup/) for how that works.

## A respectful pace

Estate cleanouts are often an emotional process, not just a job to check off a list. Drivers work at a respectful, unhurried pace — let the driver know if you need extra time during loading.

## Price example

An estate cleanout is priced per load, the same as any junk removal job — see [what a dump run costs](/pricing/cost-of-a-dump-run/) for the formula.

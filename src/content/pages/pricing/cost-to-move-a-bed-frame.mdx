## Disassembly

Most frames come apart into a headboard, footboard, and side rails. Disassembling before pickup is faster to load and reduces the risk of damage in transit — bring the hardware in a labeled bag so it isn't lost.

## Headboard sizes

Queen and king headboards are the bulkiest single piece — that's usually what determines the tier, more than the frame itself. Most disassembled frames fit [[TIER_2]].

## Do you need a helper?

A queen or king headboard can be awkward for one person to carry alone — add a helper if yours is large or heavy.

## What to prep

Disassemble what you can, bag the hardware, and measure doorways for the headboard specifically — it's usually the widest single piece.

Staying active means keeping your rating above [[RATING_THRESHOLD]] — communication, careful handling, and being on time are what riders consistently reward.

## The rating threshold

Drivers whose rating falls below [[RATING_THRESHOLD]] are removed from active status. See [driver vetting](/trust/driver-vetting/) for the full standard drivers are held to.

## What earns tips

Riders consistently mention the same things: showing up when you said you would, communicating if you're running late, and handling their items carefully. None of it is complicated — it's consistency that riders notice and reward.

## What commonly lowers a rating

- Being late without a heads-up message.
- Careless handling that scuffs or damages an item.
- Being hard to reach during the haul when a rider tries to message you.

## Staying ahead of it

Message riders proactively if plans change, and treat every item like it's fragile even when it's sturdy — both go a long way toward a consistent rating.

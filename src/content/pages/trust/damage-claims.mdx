If an item is damaged during a haul, file a claim in the app with photos — here's the full process.

## The claim window

File within [[CLAIM_WINDOW]] of the haul. Photograph the damage before moving or attempting to fix the item.

## What photos to include

Photos of the damage itself, the item's overall condition, and — if you have them — photos from before the haul for comparison.

## Who pays

If the claim is approved, payment comes from the [insurance policy](/trust/insurance/) covering the haul, up to the coverage limit. Pre-existing damage isn't covered.

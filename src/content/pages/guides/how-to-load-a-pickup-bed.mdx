Loading a pickup bed well comes down to weight distribution: heaviest items low and forward, lighter items filling the gaps on top.

[PHOTO: pickup bed loaded with heavy items low and forward, lighter items secured on top]

## Heaviest items first, low and forward

Load the heaviest items low in the bed and toward the front, near the cab, for the best weight distribution and control.

## Build from the floor up

Stack lighter, more stable items on top of heavier, flatter pieces — not the other way around.

## Keep weight centered

Distribute weight evenly from side to side rather than piling everything on one side of the bed.

## Fill gaps to prevent shifting

Use soft items — blankets, boxes — to fill gaps between larger pieces so nothing has room to shift during the drive.

## Strap before closing up

Secure everything with straps before closing the tailgate, and double-check that nothing can slide out in transit.

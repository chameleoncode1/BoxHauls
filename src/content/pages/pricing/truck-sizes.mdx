BoxHauls runs three truck tiers — [[TIER_1]], [[TIER_2]], and [[TIER_3]] — and the tier you need depends on the size and weight of what's moving, not the number of items.

## [[TIER_1]] (SUV / small bed)

An SUV or small-bed pickup. Fits boxes, small furniture like end tables and chairs, and compact appliances such as microwaves. Best for single-item store or Marketplace pickups that don't need a full-size bed.

Exact bed length and payload: [[TIER_1_DIMENSIONS]].

## [[TIER_2]] (standard half-ton, 5.5–6.5 ft bed)

A standard half-ton pickup with a 5.5–6.5 ft bed. This is the tier most single-item hauls use — couches, twin-to-queen mattresses, dressers, and dining tables.

Exact bed length and payload: [[TIER_2_DIMENSIONS]].

## [[TIER_3]] (3/4-ton, long bed, or trailer)

A 3/4-ton truck, long bed, or trailer. Used for refrigerators, washers and dryers, sectionals, and other heavy or bulky loads. Items over [[MAX_ITEM_WEIGHT]] lb typically need this tier; BoxHauls doesn't take items over [[MAX_SAFE_WEIGHT]] lb without special equipment.

Exact bed length and payload: [[TIER_3_DIMENSIONS]].

## Which tier costs more?

All tiers share the same [[BASE_FARE]] base fare and [[PER_MILE]] per-mile rate — see the [pricing formula](/pricing/). A larger tier only costs more when the item itself triggers a fee, like the [[HEAVY_FEE]] heavy-item charge. See [every fee](/pricing/fees/) for the full list.

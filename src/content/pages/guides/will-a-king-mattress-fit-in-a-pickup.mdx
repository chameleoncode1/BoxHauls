A king mattress (76 × 80 in) fits flat in a long-bed pickup, and angled or slightly overhanging in a short-bed truck.

[PHOTO: king mattress bagged and loaded in a pickup bed]

## Dimensions

| Mattress size | L × W (in) | Typical weight |
|---|---|---|
| Twin | 75 × 38 | 50-80 lb |
| Full | 75 × 54 | 70-100 lb |
| Queen | 80 × 60 | 90-130 lb |
| King | 80 × 76 | 120-150 lb |

## Bed length matters

A standard 5.5 ft bed is 66 in — shorter than a king mattress's 80-in length, so it rides angled or with a slight tailgate overhang (with a red flag if it extends past the tailgate). A 6.5 or 8 ft bed fits it flat.

## Bagging

A mattress bag protects against dust, moisture, and road debris during open-bed transport — worth using regardless of distance.

Strapping a couch securely comes down to wrapping it first, using at least two crossed straps, and anchoring to real tie-down points.

[PHOTO: couch wrapped in a moving blanket and strapped in a pickup bed]

## Wrap it first

Cover the couch in a moving blanket before strapping — this protects the fabric from the strap digging in and from road debris during the drive.

## At least two straps, crossed

Use at least two ratchet straps, one toward each end of the couch, crossed at an angle where the bed allows it. Crossed straps resist both forward and side-to-side shifting better than straight-back straps.

## Real anchor points

Attach to the truck bed's built-in tie-down anchors — not mirrors, the tailgate hinge, or anything not rated for the load.

## Check tension early

A load can settle slightly once you're moving. Check strap tension again after the first few miles and re-tighten if needed.

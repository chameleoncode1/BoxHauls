Storage unit moving costs depend on unit size — a 5x5 usually fits in one trip, a 5x10 often needs two, and a 10x10 can need three or more.

## The formula

[[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile, per trip.

| Unit size | Typical trips | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| 5x5 | 1 | [[TIER_1]] |
| 5x10 | 1-2 | [[TIER_2]] |
| 10x10 | 3+ | [[TIER_3]] |

[[HELPER_AVAILABILITY_NOTE]]

## Load estimates

A 5x5 unit is roughly a small closet's worth — boxes and maybe a dresser. A 5x10 holds a studio's worth of furniture and boxes. A 10x10 is closer to a full one-bedroom apartment and rarely fits in fewer than three trips.

## Helper

Storage units are often packed tight, sometimes to the ceiling, and originally loaded with help you may not have on moving day. A helper makes unloading the unit significantly faster.

## What to prep

Know your unit size ahead of time, and have a rough sense of what's in the back that you can't currently see — it affects trip count.

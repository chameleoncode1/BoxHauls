Drivers keep [[DRIVER_SHARE]]% of the fare, plus 100% of tips.

## Payout math

| Trip | Fare | Your share ([[DRIVER_SHARE]]%) |
|---|---|---|
| 3 miles | $26.30 | $19.73 |
| 10 miles | $48.00 | $36.00 |
| 25 miles | $94.50 | $70.88 |

Add a helper or heavy-item fee and your share of that add-on grows the total the same way.

## Tips

100% of every tip goes to the driver — BoxHauls doesn't take a cut.

## Realistic per-hour range

Pay is per haul, not per hour, so there's no fixed hourly rate. What you actually make per hour depends on trip length, how close together hauls are, and how much time you spend waiting between jobs.

## Busy times

Demand in [[METRO]] is still building as BoxHauls launches — real patterns for busy times and zones will be shared here once there's enough trip data to be honest about them.

## Fuel and wear, honestly

Like most independent-contractor driving work, gas, maintenance, and vehicle wear come out of what you earn — they're not reimbursed separately. Factor that into whether a haul is worth accepting, especially for longer trips.

Before buying used furniture from a Marketplace listing, check for structural stability, hidden damage, odors, and pests — ideally in person or with extra photos.

## Structural stability

Gently rock the piece (in person) or ask the seller to confirm joints are solid, not glued or held together with visible repairs.

## Hidden damage

Ask for photos of the underside, back, and any areas not shown in the main listing photos — water stains, cracks, or repairs often hide there.

## Odors and pests

Smoke, mildew, or pet odors are hard to remove and worth asking about directly. Signs of pests are a hard pass.

## Ask before you commit

If you can't see something clearly in the listing, ask the seller for more photos before agreeing to buy — it's much easier to back out before pickup than after.

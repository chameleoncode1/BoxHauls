A washer and dryer pair fits together in a standard pickup bed in one trip.

[PHOTO: washer and dryer loaded side by side in a pickup bed]

## Dimensions

| Appliance | L × D × H (in) | Typical weight |
|---|---|---|
| Washing machine | 27 × 27 × 38 | 150-200 lb |
| Dryer | 27 × 27 × 38 | 100-150 lb |
| Stacked pair | 27 × 27 × 74 | 250-350 lb |

## Shipping bolts

If you have the original shipping bolts, install them before pickup — they lock the washer drum in place and prevent internal damage during transport.

## Fit

Both units together are about 54 in wide side by side, fitting within a standard truck bed's width — the driver will confirm orientation based on your specific bed length.

BoxHauls handles last-minute and same-day moves — no advance booking required, priced the same as any other haul.

## What's realistic today

Same-day availability depends on which drivers are covering [[METRO]] at the time you book. A single trip or two is realistic; a full multi-room household move on short notice usually isn't.

## Booking early in the day

The earlier in the day you book, the better the odds of same-day pickup — driver availability tightens as the day goes on.

## No surge pricing

Booking last-minute doesn't cost more — it's the same [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile as any other haul, with no surge pricing based on demand.

## Price example

A last-minute move at 10 miles runs about $48 before any helper fee — see [pricing](/pricing/) for the full formula.

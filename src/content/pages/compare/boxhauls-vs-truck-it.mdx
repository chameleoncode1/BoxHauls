Truck It is a bidding-based pickup and delivery app — you post your job and drivers bid on it, rather than getting one calculated price up front.

## How Truck It's model works

You post your item, photos, and pickup/drop-off locations; nearby drivers submit bids; you pick one and pay that flat amount, with no added mileage, fuel, or insurance charges tacked on later, by Truck It's own description.

## Coverage

Truck It markets itself as live across the United States with a growing driver network. Its Fresno- or Clovis-specific driver density isn't published — worth checking directly in the app for your date and location rather than assuming coverage.

## Where Truck It wins

A bidding model can work in your favor on a slow day if multiple drivers compete for your job, and it's available in more places nationally, at least on paper.

## Where BoxHauls wins

A known, calculated price instead of waiting on bids, and confirmed local driver coverage in [[METRO]] rather than an unverified national footprint.

## Verdict

If you want a guaranteed price without waiting on bids, and confirmed coverage in [[METRO]], book with [BoxHauls](/pricing/) directly.

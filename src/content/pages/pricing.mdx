Most BoxHauls hauls in [[METRO]] cost between $25 and $95, depending on distance, item, and whether you add a helper.

## The formula

Every price is [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile, plus item and helper fees where they apply. No hourly rate, no surge pricing based on demand.

## Worked examples

| Distance | Base fare | Mileage | Total (no add-ons) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 miles | [[BASE_FARE]] | $9.30 | $26.30 |
| 10 miles | [[BASE_FARE]] | $31.00 | $48.00 |
| 25 miles | [[BASE_FARE]] | $77.50 | $94.50 |

Add a helper ([[HELPER_FEE]]) or a heavy item ([[HEAVY_FEE]]) and the total goes up by that flat amount — the per-mile rate doesn't change.

## Tiers

- **[[TIER_1]]** — smaller items, a few boxes, single trips.
- **[[TIER_2]]** — most single-item hauls: couches, mattresses, dressers.
- **[[TIER_3]]** — appliances, heavier or bulkier loads.

See [truck sizes](/pricing/truck-sizes/) for bed length, payload, and exactly what fits in each tier.

## Fees

Heavy items add [[HEAVY_FEE]]. A helper adds [[HELPER_FEE]] — [[HELPER_AVAILABILITY_NOTE]] Stairs and wait time have their own charges — see [every fee, explained](/pricing/fees/) for the full table and what's never charged.

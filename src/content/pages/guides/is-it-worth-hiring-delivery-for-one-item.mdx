For a single item, delivery is often worth it — a truck rental's daily minimum, insurance, and your own time frequently cost more than one trip.

## The real cost of doing it yourself

A truck rental has a daily rate regardless of how long you actually need it, plus mileage, insurance, and fuel — and your own time loading, driving, and unloading.

## The cost of one BoxHauls trip

[[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile, no minimum order size — a single chair or dresser is a completely normal booking. See [single-item delivery](/services/furniture-delivery/single-item-delivery/) for how it works.

## When doing it yourself still makes sense

If you already own a truck and have the time, doing it yourself is essentially free beyond fuel. It's the rental-plus-your-time math that usually tips the scale toward delivery.

## Bottom line

For one item, compare a rental's daily minimum plus your time against a single trip's actual cost — the math usually favors delivery for anything beyond a short, easy carry.

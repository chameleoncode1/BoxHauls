BoxHauls hauls yard waste — branches, clippings, and general green waste — by the load.

## Green waste sites

Yard waste typically goes to a dedicated green waste facility rather than a standard landfill — this keeps the material out of the regular waste stream.

## Branch length

Cut and bundle branches where you can before pickup — shorter, bundled pieces load more efficiently than long, loose branches sticking out of the bed.

## Bagging

Bag loose material like leaves, clippings, and small debris. It loads faster and stays contained for the drive.

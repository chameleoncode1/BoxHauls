Most dishwasher moves in [[METRO]] cost between $26 and $95, depending on distance — [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile.

## The formula

[[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile. A dishwasher doesn't add its own fee.

| Distance | Base price | With a helper (+ [[HELPER_FEE]]) |
|---|---|---|
| 3 miles | $26.30 | $43.30 |
| 10 miles | $48.00 | $65.00 |
| 25 miles | $94.50 | $111.50 |

## Dishwasher specs

Most dishwashers weigh 75-125 lb and fit [[TIER_1]] or [[TIER_2]].

## Disconnect required

Water and power lines need to be disconnected before pickup — that's a job for you or a plumber ahead of time, not something the driver does. See [appliance delivery](/services/appliance-delivery/) for what drivers do and don't handle.

## What to prep

Disconnect the water and power lines, and have the dishwasher clear of the cabinet opening before the driver arrives.

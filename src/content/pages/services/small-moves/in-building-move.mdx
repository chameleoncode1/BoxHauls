BoxHauls handles moves within the same building or across the street — where the drive is short but the carrying isn't.

## Labor-only option

When there's barely any real driving distance — moving between units in the same building, or across the street — ask about a labor-only option instead of the standard per-mile haul.

## Between floors

Moving between floors in the same building, by stairs or elevator, is a common in-building job. Let the driver know the floor and access details when you book.

## When a truck still makes sense

If you're moving across the street but have a full load, a truck trip may still be faster than multiple hand-carried runs — ask which option fits your load when you book.

## Price example

A short in-building move is often priced differently than a standard haul — ask for a quote based on your specific distance and load when you book. See [pricing](/pricing/) for the standard per-trip formula.

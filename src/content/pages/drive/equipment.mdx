The basic kit every hauling driver should carry, with rough costs to gear up.

## Buy list

| Item | Typical cost |
|---|---|
| Ratchet straps (4-pack) | $20-40 |
| Moving blankets (set of 4-6) | $30-60 |
| Furniture dolly | $30-80 |
| Loading ramp | $50-150 |
| Tarp | $15-30 |
| Work gloves | $10-20 |

A basic kit runs roughly $150-350 total, depending on quality. Ratchet straps and blankets matter most — they're what keeps a customer's item from shifting or getting damaged in transit.

## Ratchet straps

Secure every load before you drive — loose items are both a damage risk and a safety hazard on the road.

## Moving blankets

Protect furniture surfaces and cushion contact points, especially for open-bed transport.

## Dolly and ramp

A furniture dolly and loading ramp make heavy items — appliances, safes — manageable without straining your back.

## Tarp and gloves

A tarp protects items from weather in an open bed; gloves protect your hands and improve grip on heavy or awkward loads.

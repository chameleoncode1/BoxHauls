BoxHauls picks up large Costco purchases — furniture, TVs, mattress-in-a-box — and brings them home the same day.

## How warehouse pickup works

Costco warehouse purchases are picked up from the floor or a designated pickup area, not shipped to your door by default for most large items. Give the driver your order details when you book.

## Membership card rule

An active Costco membership is required to shop the warehouse floor. Some prepaid online orders have separate pickup rules — check your order confirmation for specifics.

## What's typically picked up

TVs, furniture, and mattress-in-a-box are among the most common large Costco items BoxHauls hauls.

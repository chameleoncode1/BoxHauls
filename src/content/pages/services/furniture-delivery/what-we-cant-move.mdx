BoxHauls is honest about what a truck and driver can't safely handle — here's the full list, and where to go instead.

## Grand pianos

Upright pianos may be possible depending on the driver's equipment and experience. Grand pianos need a specialized piano mover — the weight distribution and crating requirements are beyond what a truck-and-driver haul is built for.

## Hazardous materials

Paint, chemicals, propane tanks, and similar hazardous materials aren't accepted. Contact your local transfer station or a hazmat disposal service instead.

## Vehicles

BoxHauls moves items, not vehicles — motorcycles, cars, and similar need a tow service or specialized transport, not a pickup-truck bed.

## Heavy items without equipment

Items over [[MAX_ITEM_WEIGHT]] lb typically need [[TIER_3]] and a helper. BoxHauls doesn't take items over [[MAX_SAFE_WEIGHT]] lb without equipment — like a dolly rated for that weight or a ramp — that a given driver may not have. Ask before booking if you're near that limit.

## Live animals

BoxHauls moves items, not people or live animals.

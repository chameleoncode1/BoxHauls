Most one-bedroom apartment moves in [[METRO]] run $90-$280, typically across two trips.

## How many trips?

A full one-bedroom — bed, dresser, couch, dining table, boxes — usually needs two trips in [[TIER_2]] or [[TIER_3]]. A larger amount of furniture, or a second bedroom used for storage, can push it to three.

## When to add a second truck

If you're on a moving-day deadline and don't want to wait between trips, ask about booking a second truck to run at the same time instead of back-to-back trips.

## When movers are cheaper

If you have a large amount of furniture or need a crew to carry heavy pieces together (a china cabinet, a sectional), a full moving company charging by the hour may work out cheaper than several BoxHauls trips. See [BoxHauls vs. hiring movers](/compare/boxhauls-vs-hiring-movers/) for a side-by-side.

## What to prep

Break down furniture where you can, box up everything loose, and stage items near the door before the driver arrives.

A refrigerator can often be loaded solo with a dolly and ramp on flat ground — the moment stairs or lifting enters the picture, that's the line where solo loading stops being safe.

[PHOTO: refrigerator being rolled up a ramp on a hand truck]

## When solo works

Flat ground, a ramp to the truck bed, and a furniture dolly under the unit — rolling a refrigerator this way keeps the weight off your back and is realistic for one person.

## When it's not

Stairs, a curb with no ramp access, or any point where you'd need to lift rather than roll the unit — that's the signal to stop and request a helper rather than risk an injury or a dropped appliance.

## Check the job details first

If the customer requested a helper, that's visible in the job details before you accept — you'll know upfront whether you're expected to handle it solo or with a second person. See [drive for BoxHauls](/drive/) for how job details arrive.

## Gear that helps

A furniture dolly and loading ramp are the two pieces of equipment that make the biggest difference for solo appliance loads — see [driver equipment](/drive/equipment/) for the full kit.

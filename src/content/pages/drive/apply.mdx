Apply through the BoxHauls app — here's what happens after you submit.

## How to apply

Download the app and complete the driver application: truck details, driver's license, and insurance information.

## What happens next

Your background check runs through [[BACKGROUND_PROVIDER]] — identity, license, driving record, and criminal background — and your vehicle is checked against the [tier requirements](/drive/requirements/). See [how BoxHauls checks drivers and trucks](/trust/driver-vetting/) for the full process.

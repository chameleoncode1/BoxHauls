A BoxHauls dump run hauls your load to the transfer station — you load it or ask about load-assist, and the gate fee passes through at cost.

## You load, or we load

Have your load ready at the curb before the driver arrives, or ask about load-assist when you book.

## Transfer stations

American Avenue Disposal Site and the Cedar Avenue Recycling & Transfer Station (CARTS) are the two main options in [[METRO]] — see [dump fees in [[METRO]]](/guides/dump-fees-in-fresno/) for current hours and fees.

## Fee pass-through

The gate fee is charged exactly as billed at the transfer station — BoxHauls doesn't mark it up.

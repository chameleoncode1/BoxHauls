## Dresser specs

Most dressers weigh 100-250 lb depending on size and material. Fits [[TIER_2]] for standard 6-drawer dressers.

## Drawers out or taped

Remove drawers or tape them shut before the move — an unsecured drawer can slide open and add weight and imbalance to the carry.

## Do you need a helper?

For stairs, yes — a loaded dresser is heavy and awkward on a staircase even after emptying the drawers.

## What to prep

Empty or tape the drawers, measure doorways, and clear a path — especially for any stairs involved.

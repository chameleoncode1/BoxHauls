Keeping a mattress clean during transport comes down to one thing: bag it before it goes anywhere near an open truck bed.

[PHOTO: mattress in a sealed protective bag loaded in a pickup bed]

## Bag it

A mattress bag protects against dust, moisture, and road debris — the single most effective step for open-bed transport.

## Seal the bag

Tape the bag's opening shut so it doesn't work loose or flap open during the drive.

## Keep it off the bed floor

Where space allows, keep the mattress from lying directly against a dirty bed liner — even bagged, it helps.

## Strap, don't fold

Secure the mattress flat or angled with straps. Folding it can damage the internal springs or foam structure.

## Unbag it indoors

Remove the bag once the mattress is inside to avoid trapping moisture against the fabric.

Property managers can offer tenants a moving option and handle unit turnovers, both through BoxHauls.

## Two ways this helps

- **Tenant moves** — offer BoxHauls as a moving option for tenants moving in or out, especially for smaller units or single-item moves.
- **Unit turnovers** — book your own hauls to clear left-behind furniture between tenants. See [property manager unit turnovers](/services/business-hauling/property-manager-turnovers/) for tight 48-hour-window details.

## Recurring accounts

Managing multiple units or properties? See [business hauling](/services/business-hauling/) for recurring-account and invoicing options.

## Pricing

Tenant-paid moves use the standard BoxHauls rate — [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile. Turnover hauls you book yourself follow the same formula, billed to your account.

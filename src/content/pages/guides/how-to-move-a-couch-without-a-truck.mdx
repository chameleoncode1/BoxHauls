You don't need to own a truck to move a couch — measure first, then rent one, borrow one, or book a driver who already has one.

## Measure first

Measure the couch's length, depth, and height, then measure every doorway, hallway, and stairwell it has to pass through — including diagonal clearance for turns. Most couches run 72-96 in long; see [will it fit in a pickup bed](/guides/will-it-fit-in-a-pickup-bed/) for more sizes.

## Your options

- **Rent a truck** — cheapest per hour, but you're driving, loading, and unloading it yourself.
- **Ask a friend** — free if someone has a truck and the time, but scheduling depends on their availability.
- **Book a truck and driver** — see [couch delivery](/services/furniture-delivery/couch-delivery/) for a driver who handles the truck and the loading.

## Protect it

Wrap the couch in moving blankets or plastic wrap to protect fabric and cushions, especially for open-bed transport.

## Secure the load

Use ratchet straps to keep the couch from shifting during the drive — this matters even more in an open truck bed than in an enclosed van.

## Get help for the heavy part

A couch is awkward for one person alone, especially on stairs or through a tight doorway. Add a second person if either applies.

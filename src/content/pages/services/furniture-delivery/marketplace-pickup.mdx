## Safety

Send the driver the listing link and the seller's phone number when you book. Meeting in a public place for the handoff is safest, where the listing allows it.

## What the driver checks

The driver confirms the item matches your description before loading it, but they're not an appraiser — inspect listing photos closely before you buy.

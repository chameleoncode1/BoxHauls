Offer BoxHauls as a delivery option at checkout — customers get same-day delivery, you don't have to run a delivery fleet.

## How it works at checkout

A customer buys something too big to carry home, and instead of quoting your own delivery window or turning away the sale, you point them to BoxHauls — a QR code at the register, a link on your order confirmation, or your staff booking it directly on their behalf. The customer enters their address, sees the price, and books.

## What the customer pays

The same [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile as any other BoxHauls haul — see [pricing](/pricing/). There's no special partner markup; the customer pays what any BoxHauls customer pays.

## What your store gets

A sale that doesn't fall through because a customer doesn't own a truck, without owning, insuring, or scheduling a delivery fleet yourself. You're not paying BoxHauls to offer this — the customer pays for their own delivery.

Appliance dealers can offer same-day delivery at checkout, with old-unit haul-away in the same trip, without running a delivery fleet.

## The pitch

A customer buying a refrigerator or washer often needs it home the same day, and may also need the old one hauled away. BoxHauls covers both without your store scheduling either.

## Checkout flow

A QR code at the register, a link on the receipt or order confirmation, or staff booking it directly for the customer.

## Two-person handling and haul-away

Appliances are heavy — a helper add-on is available for two-person handling, and haul-away for the old unit can be booked in the same trip. See [appliance delivery](/services/appliance-delivery/) for the full details.

## Pricing

Customers pay the standard BoxHauls rate — [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile. No cost to your store.

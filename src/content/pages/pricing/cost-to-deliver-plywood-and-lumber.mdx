## Flat or angled

Standard 4x8 sheets usually lay flat in a pickup bed. If the stack is too tall to lay flat, sheets can angle against the cab — let the driver know your sheet count when you book so they can plan the load.

## Overhang

Lumber longer than the truck bed overhangs the tailgate. When it does, a red flag (or reflective marker after dark) goes on the overhanging end.

## Tier

A standard load of sheets or dimensional lumber fits [[TIER_2]]. A full truckload of building material may need [[TIER_3]].

## What to prep

Have your material stacked and counted before pickup, and separate any pieces that need extra care (warped, split, or fragile trim).

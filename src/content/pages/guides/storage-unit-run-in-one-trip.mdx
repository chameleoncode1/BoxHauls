Fitting a storage unit into one trip depends on size — a 5x5 or 5x10 often works, a 10x10 usually doesn't.

## Know your unit size first

A 5x5 unit is roughly a small closet's worth. A 5x10 holds a studio's worth of furniture and boxes. A 10x10 is closer to a full one-bedroom and rarely fits in one trip — see [cost to move a storage unit](/pricing/cost-to-move-a-storage-unit/) for trip estimates by size.

## Loading order

Load heaviest, flattest items first — furniture pieces — then fill in with boxes, mirroring how you'd load a truck bed for weight distribution.

## What's often forgotten

Units are frequently packed to the ceiling, with items in the back you haven't seen in a while. Factor that into your trip-count estimate, not just what's visible from the door.

## A helper makes it faster

Storage units are often packed tight and loaded without help originally — a helper speeds up the unload significantly. [[HELPER_AVAILABILITY_NOTE]]

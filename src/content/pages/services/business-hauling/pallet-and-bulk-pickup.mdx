BoxHauls picks up pallets and bulk loads — forklift-to-bed loading, no liftgate.

## No liftgate

BoxHauls trucks don't have a liftgate. Pallets load onto the truck bed by forklift at pickup — plan for forklift access at drop-off too, or be ready to break the pallet down for hand unloading.

## Weight per pallet

Pallet weight varies widely by contents. Confirm the approximate weight when you book so the driver brings a truck rated for the load — see [truck sizes](/pricing/truck-sizes/).

BoxHauls picks up from Ashley Furniture, whether it's a boxed piece or a fully assembled outlet find.

## Outlet pickup

Ashley's outlet section often has clearance and floor-model pieces available for immediate pickup rather than special order.

## Boxed vs. assembled

Some Ashley furniture ships boxed and ready to assemble; other pieces are fully assembled at purchase. Confirm which applies to your order before booking so the driver knows what to expect and whether a helper makes sense.

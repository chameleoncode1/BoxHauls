BoxHauls delivers couches and sofas from a store, a Marketplace seller, or between your own addresses — with the price shown before you book.

## Sizes

Most couches are 72–96 in long and 100–200 lb, fitting [[TIER_2]]. Sectionals and larger pieces may need [[TIER_3]] — see [truck sizes](/pricing/truck-sizes/).

## Doorways and stairs

Measure the doorway, any hallway turns, and stairwells before booking. Add a helper for stairs or a tight doorway.

## Wrapping

The driver wraps and secures the couch for open-bed transport, protecting cushions and fabric from the elements.

## Price example

A 10-mile couch delivery runs about $48 before any helper fee — see [cost to move a couch](/pricing/cost-to-move-a-couch/) for the full formula and distance table.

BoxHauls hauls an old mattress to the dump or a recycling facility, whichever fits.

## Recycling programs

Many mattresses can be recycled rather than landfilled — see [mattress recycling in [[METRO]]](/guides/mattress-recycling-in-fresno/) for current local options.

## Bag requirement

Many transfer stations and recycling programs require mattresses to be bagged, particularly if there's any sign of pests — check current requirements at your destination facility before your trip.

## Price example

A single-mattress dump run at 10 miles runs about a quarter-load rate plus the gate fee — see [what a dump run costs](/pricing/cost-of-a-dump-run/) for the full breakdown.

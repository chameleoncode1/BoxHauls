Most studio apartment moves in [[METRO]] cost between $60 and $180, usually in one or two trips depending on how much you have.

## The formula

[[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile, per trip. A second trip adds another base fare.

| Distance | One trip | Two trips |
|---|---|---|
| 3 miles | $26.30 | $52.60 |
| 10 miles | $48.00 | $96.00 |
| 25 miles | $94.50 | $189.00 |

## Will it fit in one trip?

A typical studio — bed frame and mattress, dresser, a small couch or table, a handful of boxes — usually fits [[TIER_2]] in one trip. If you have more furniture or a lot of boxes, plan for two trips.

## Boxes

As a rough guide, a full [[TIER_1]] load holds roughly 15-20 medium boxes on their own, with no furniture. Mixing boxes and furniture reduces that count.

## Helper

Most people moving a studio are doing it alone. A helper makes loading and carrying significantly faster.

## What to prep

Break down the bed frame if possible, tape and label boxes, and clear a path from your door to the curb.

College move-out week is one of the busiest local periods for demand — book as early as you know your date, since pricing doesn't change but availability does.

## Why booking early matters

Move-out week compresses a huge number of moves into just a few days across [[METRO]]'s campuses. Same-day availability depends on driver coverage at the time you book, and that coverage gets scarcer as the week gets busier — booking early is about securing a driver, not about price.

## Pricing stays the same

[[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile — the same formula as any other week, with no move-out-week surcharge.

## If your exact time isn't set yet

Book with a rough window as soon as you can, and refine the details closer to the date. Early booking is about getting on a driver's schedule, not locking in an exact hour immediately.

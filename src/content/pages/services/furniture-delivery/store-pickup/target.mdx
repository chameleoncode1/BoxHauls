BoxHauls picks up furniture and large purchases from Target, including orders too big for Drive Up.

## Drive Up limits

Target's Drive Up service has size and weight limits meant for smaller orders — larger furniture typically needs in-store pickup or guest services instead. Check your order confirmation for which applies.

## Furniture pickup

Target carries a growing furniture selection — sized for a real truck bed, not a car trunk.

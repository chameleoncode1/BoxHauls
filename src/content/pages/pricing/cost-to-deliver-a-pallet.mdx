Most single-pallet deliveries in [[METRO]] cost between $26 and $95, depending on distance.

## The formula

[[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile.

| Distance | Base price | With a helper (+ [[HELPER_FEE]]) |
|---|---|---|
| 3 miles | $26.30 | $43.30 |
| 10 miles | $48.00 | $65.00 |
| 25 miles | $94.50 | $111.50 |

[[HELPER_AVAILABILITY_NOTE]]

## No liftgate

BoxHauls trucks don't have a liftgate. A palletized load needs to be loaded onto the truck bed by forklift at pickup. At drop-off, plan for forklift access again, or be ready to break the pallet down and unload by hand.

## Tier

[[TIER_2]] or [[TIER_3]], depending on pallet weight — confirm the approximate weight when you book.

## For repeat pallet runs

If you're moving pallets regularly for a business, see [business hauling](/services/business-hauling/) for recurring-pickup options.

Confirm forklift access at both ends before booking, and have the pallet weight ready.

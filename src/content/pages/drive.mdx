Own a pickup truck? Drive for BoxHauls and keep [[DRIVER_SHARE]]% of every fare, plus 100% of tips.

## Earnings

Drivers keep [[DRIVER_SHARE]]% of the fare — BoxHauls doesn't take a cut of tips. See [how much BoxHauls drivers make](/drive/earnings/) for worked examples by trip length.

## Requirements

A pickup truck in good condition, a valid license, insurance, and a background check. See [driver requirements](/drive/requirements/) for the full list.

## How jobs arrive

Job offers come through the app with pickup, drop-off, and item details. You choose whether to accept before you commit — nothing is assigned automatically.

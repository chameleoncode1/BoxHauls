BoxHauls insures your item while it's in the truck — here's the difference between what's covered and what isn't.

## What's covered

Damage that happens while your item is loaded, in transit, or being unloaded by the driver is covered under BoxHauls's insurance — see [insurance](/trust/insurance/) for the specific coverage and limits.

## What's typically not covered

Pre-existing damage, and damage from packing decisions you made yourself before the driver arrived, generally fall outside coverage. Anything unclear is worth asking about directly.

## Filing a claim

Report any damage as soon as possible after the haul — see [what to do if an item is damaged](/guides/what-to-do-if-an-item-is-damaged/) for the exact steps and timeline.

## Why this matters beyond BoxHauls

Not every delivery option carries the same coverage — see [trust & safety](/trust/) for how BoxHauls's insurance compares to going without any coverage at all.

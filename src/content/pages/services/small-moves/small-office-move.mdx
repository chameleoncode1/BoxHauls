BoxHauls moves small offices — desks, chairs, filing cabinets, and equipment — priced per trip, not by a moving crew's hourly rate.

## What fits a small office move

Desks, office chairs, filing cabinets, small conference tables, and boxed supplies. Most single-office loads fit [[TIER_2]] or [[TIER_3]].

## After-hours moves

Some offices require moving outside business hours. Ask about after-hours or weekend availability when you book — it depends on driver coverage at that time.

## Elevators and building access

If your building requires an elevator reservation or has loading-dock rules, let the driver know when you book so they can plan around it.

A small office move at 10 miles runs about $48 in [[TIER_2]] before any helper fee — see [pricing](/pricing/) for the full formula.

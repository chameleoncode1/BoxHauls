If a Marketplace seller doesn't show up at pickup, the driver waits a reasonable amount of time, then the haul ends if there's no response.

## Before you book

Confirm the exact pickup time and address directly with the seller before booking — a vague "sometime this afternoon" window makes a no-show more likely.

## If the seller doesn't answer

The driver will wait a reasonable amount of time at the pickup location and attempt to reach both you and the seller. If there's still no response, the pickup can't happen.

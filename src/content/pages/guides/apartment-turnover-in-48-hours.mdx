Turning a unit around in 48 hours is realistic if left-behind items are cleared quickly — the biggest time sink is usually waiting on a truck, not the cleanout itself.

## Assess and sort immediately

Walk the unit and sort left-behind items into keep, donate, and dump piles right away. Sorting on the spot, rather than ahead of time, is what most often blows a 48-hour window.

## Book loads as they're ready

Rather than waiting to consolidate everything into one trip, book each load as it's ready — a dump run, a donation drop-off, anything going to storage — as separate hauls.

## Recurring accounts speed this up

Property managers turning multiple units regularly benefit from a standing account rather than booking each turnover from scratch — see [property manager unit turnovers](/services/business-hauling/property-manager-turnovers/) for account and invoicing options.

## What to have ready

Know roughly what's in the unit before the driver arrives — furniture count, whether anything is oversized — so the driver brings the right tier on the first trip.

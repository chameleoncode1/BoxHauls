BoxHauls hauls away a full garage cleanout — one load or several, priced per load.

## Sorting

Sort into keep, donate, and dump piles before the driver arrives, so the load and price are clear from the start.

## Hazmat exclusions

Paint, chemicals, batteries, propane tanks, and similar hazardous materials aren't accepted at the landfill and need separate disposal.

## Multi-load cleanouts

A bigger cleanout than one truck bed can hold? Book multiple loads — each is priced the same way.

Found it on OfferUp? Send the driver the listing link and the seller's number, and they handle the pickup.

## How it works

Message the seller through the OfferUp app to confirm the item and arrange a meeting time, then book the haul and send the driver the listing link and the seller's phone number.

## Meeting-point rules

Meet in a public, well-lit location where the listing allows it — safest for both driver and seller.

## What to send the driver

The listing link and the seller's phone number.

A 4x8 plywood sheet doesn't fit flat in a short-bed pickup — it needs to angle against the wheel wells or overhang the tailgate with a red flag.

[PHOTO: plywood sheets angled in a pickup bed with a red flag on the overhang]

## Bed lengths vs. sheet length

| Bed length | Fits 8 ft sheet flat? |
|---|---|
| 5.5 ft (66 in) | No — angle or overhang required |
| 6.5 ft (78 in) | Angled, minimal overhang |
| 8 ft (96 in) | Yes, flat with tailgate up |

## Overhang and the red flag

If the sheet overhangs the tailgate, a red flag (or reflective marker after dark) is required on the overhanging end — standard for any load extending past the bed.

## Multiple sheets

A stack of several sheets fits [[TIER_2]] in most cases. Mention your sheet count when you book so the driver brings the right tier and plans the load.

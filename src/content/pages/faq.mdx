Quick answers below — each links to the page that covers it in full.

## Booking

**How do I book a haul?** Enter your pickup and drop-off addresses, pick your item and truck tier, and confirm once you see the price. See [how it works](/how-it-works/).

## Pricing

**How much does it cost?** [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile, plus any item or helper fees. See [pricing](/pricing/) for worked examples.

**Is BoxHauls available outside [[METRO]]?** BoxHauls currently serves [[METRO]] within a [[RADIUS]]-mile radius. See [where BoxHauls operates](/cities/).

## Safety

**Is BoxHauls insured?** Yes — every haul is covered while it's in the truck. See [trust & safety](/trust/).

**Are drivers background checked?** Yes, every driver clears a [[BACKGROUND_PROVIDER]] background check before their first haul. See [driver vetting](/trust/driver-vetting/).

## Drivers

**Can I make money driving for BoxHauls?** If you own a pickup truck, yes — BoxHauls pays drivers a share of the fare plus 100% of tips. Look for the driver section in the footer for requirements and how payouts work.

## Business

**Does BoxHauls work with businesses?** Yes — see [business & jobsite hauling](/services/business-hauling/) for jobsite runs and retail last-mile, or [partners](/partners/) to offer BoxHauls delivery at your store.

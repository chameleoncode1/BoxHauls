BoxHauls delivers dishwashers and ranges — with gas safety handled by a licensed pro before the driver ever touches the unit.

## Gas safety

If your range is gas-powered, the line needs to be disconnected by a licensed plumber before pickup, and reconnected the same way after delivery. Drivers don't handle gas line work — it's not safe or legal for anyone but a licensed pro.

## Dishwasher hookup

The driver delivers and places the dishwasher, but plumbing and electrical hookup need a licensed installer — have that arranged separately.

TaskRabbit is a general task marketplace, not a dedicated truck-and-driver service — its Fresno presence is confirmed, but it's built around hourly-rate helpers, not a per-trip truck haul.

## What TaskRabbit actually offers here

TaskRabbit has a Fresno location page with categories for delivery, furniture assembly, handyman work, errands, and yard work. Its Fresno delivery-service listing is framed around general courier and errand tasks — not a specialized moving service with dedicated trucks, by TaskRabbit's own description. Individual Taskers may offer truck-based moving help as a separate, per-Tasker service, but availability specifically for large-item moves in [[METRO]] isn't confirmed.

## Where TaskRabbit wins

A much broader range of task categories beyond moving — useful if you need furniture assembly or handyman work in the same booking flow.

## Where BoxHauls wins

A dedicated truck-and-driver service built specifically for moving items between two addresses, with a flat price shown up front instead of an open-ended hourly rate.

## Verdict

For assembly or general errands, TaskRabbit's broader marketplace makes sense. For getting one or more items from one [[METRO]] address to another, BoxHauls is purpose-built for that — see [pricing](/pricing/).

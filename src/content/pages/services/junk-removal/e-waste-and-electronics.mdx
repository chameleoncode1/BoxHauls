BoxHauls hauls TVs, monitors, and other electronics to e-waste recycling — not a standard landfill.

## What's accepted

TVs, monitors, computers, printers, and similar electronics. These go to a dedicated e-waste recycling facility rather than general disposal.

## Recycling fees

Some e-waste facilities charge a separate recycling fee for certain items, passed through at cost the same way a landfill gate fee is — the driver will confirm before drop-off.

## Data note

Wipe personal data from any device with storage — computers, phones, external drives — before pickup. BoxHauls transports the device; it doesn't handle data destruction or wiping.

A load of electronics at 10 miles runs a per-load rate plus any recycling fee — see [what a dump run costs](/pricing/cost-of-a-dump-run/) for the formula.

Curri isn't really a competitor to BoxHauls for a typical move — it's a business-to-business delivery platform built for construction and industrial supply deliveries, not for individuals moving furniture or household items.

## What Curri actually does

Curri connects construction suppliers and contractors with a driver network for same-day, rush, and scheduled jobsite deliveries — building materials like roofing, HVAC, electrical, and plumbing supplies, using cars, trucks, and flatbeds. It also offers an API and carrier-management tools built for businesses, not a consumer booking flow.

## Different audiences, different jobs

If you're moving a couch, a mattress, or clearing out an apartment, Curri isn't built for that use case. If you're a contractor needing material delivered to a jobsite regularly, see [business hauling](/services/business-hauling/) for BoxHauls's own contractor-facing option instead — a more directly comparable service for that specific need.

Contractors and supply houses can offer BoxHauls as a delivery option at checkout, or book their own recurring material runs.

## The pitch

A customer buying lumber, fixtures, or building material without a truck of their own is a common lost or delayed sale — offering BoxHauls at checkout keeps that sale moving the same day.

## For your own jobsite runs

Book BoxHauls yourself for recurring supply pickups and jobsite deliveries — see [jobsite material runs](/services/business-hauling/jobsite-material-runs/) for overhang rules and same-day availability.

## Recurring accounts

See [business hauling](/services/business-hauling/) for recurring-account and invoicing options built for contractors and suppliers.

## Pricing

Customer-paid deliveries use the standard BoxHauls rate — [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile. No cost to your business for offering it as an option.

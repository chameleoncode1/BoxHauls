Flat-pack furniture is the easier choice for pickup-truck transport — the boxes stack efficiently, while assembled pieces take up more room and are harder to protect in an open bed.

## Why flat-pack travels better

Flat boxes stack tightly and protect their contents inside factory packaging — far more space-efficient than the same piece already assembled, and less exposed to weather in an open bed.

## When assembled makes sense anyway

If you're buying from a store's floor model or a piece that's only sold assembled, that's simply what you're working with — see [store pickup](/services/furniture-delivery/store-pickup/) for how pickup works either way.

## No IKEA store in [[METRO]]

There's no IKEA location in [[METRO]] itself — see [IKEA pickup](/services/furniture-delivery/store-pickup/ikea/) for what that actually means for getting IKEA furniture home.

## Assembly isn't included

BoxHauls delivers and places furniture but doesn't assemble it. If you'd rather skip assembly, buying pre-assembled where available is an option — just expect a bulkier haul.

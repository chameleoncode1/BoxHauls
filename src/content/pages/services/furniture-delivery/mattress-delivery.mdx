BoxHauls delivers mattresses from a store, a Marketplace seller, or between your own addresses — always transported upright, always with the price shown before you book.

## Mattress bag

A mattress bag is recommended, especially for open-bed transport, to keep it clean and dry in transit.

## Upright transport

Mattresses travel upright, never folded or bent, to avoid damaging the internal coils or foam.

## Box springs

A box spring travels as part of the same haul — mention it when you book so the driver knows to expect two pieces.

## Price example

A 10-mile mattress delivery runs about $48 before any helper fee — see [cost to move a mattress](/pricing/cost-to-move-a-mattress/) for sizes, weights, and the full formula.

If you're comparing Bungii as a consumer option, the short version is: you can't book it anymore. Bungii shut down its consumer app around August 2024 and now serves large retailers and distributors exclusively, not individuals.

## What Bungii actually is now

By Bungii's own description, it's no longer "a consumer app or small business courier." The company pivoted to business-to-business final-mile delivery — moving high volumes of oversized items for retailers, distributors, manufacturers, and third-party delivery companies. Bungii's Fresno city page still exists, but it's framed entirely around B2B "Ad-Hoc" and "Dedicated" delivery service, not something you can book as an individual moving a couch.

## Why this page still exists

Bungii used to be a well-known consumer name in this category, and some readers searching for it are trying to find out whether it's still an option — it isn't, for individual moves.

Most sectional moves in [[METRO]] cost between $26 and $95, depending on distance — [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile, plus a helper fee since sectionals are always a two-person job.

## The formula

[[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile. A helper is effectively required for this haul.

| Distance | Base price | With a helper (+ [[HELPER_FEE]]) |
|---|---|---|
| 3 miles | $26.30 | $43.30 |
| 10 miles | $48.00 | $65.00 |
| 25 miles | $94.50 | $111.50 |

[[HELPER_AVAILABILITY_NOTE]]

## Sectional specs

Most sectionals break into 2-5 pieces totaling 250-450 lb. Standard sets fit [[TIER_2]]; larger 4-5 piece sets may need [[TIER_3]]. See [truck sizes](/pricing/truck-sizes/).

## Do you need a helper?

Yes — a sectional's size and weight make it a two-person job on essentially every haul, not just for stairs or tight doorways.

## What to prep

Remove any loose cushions and measure doorways at both ends — sectionals are bulkier than a standard couch and more likely to need an angled carry.

Of the national on-demand truck apps, GoShare is the one that's actually confirmed to operate in [[METRO]] and [[NEIGHBORHOOD]] as of August 2026 — making this the most directly relevant comparison in this cluster.

## Coverage

GoShare has dedicated service pages for both Fresno and Clovis, along with nearby cities like Fowler, Madera, Sanger, and Selma — a real, current local presence, not just a nationwide footprint that happens to include a ZIP code here.

## Where GoShare wins

A broader vehicle lineup (courier cars up through box trucks) and a wider national network beyond just [[METRO]], plus an established mobile app today.

## Where BoxHauls wins

A single, simple flat-rate formula shown before you book, with no separate hourly-vs-per-mile decision to make, and local-only focus that keeps driver coverage concentrated in [[METRO]] and [[NEIGHBORHOOD]] rather than spread thin nationally.

## Verdict

Both operate here — GoShare is a reasonable option if you want more vehicle-size choices or need a specific hourly rate; BoxHauls is built around one simple per-trip price for exactly this kind of local haul. See [pricing](/pricing/) for the full formula.

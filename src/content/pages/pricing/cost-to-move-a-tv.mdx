## Box or blanket

The original box is ideal, but a moving blanket works if you don't have it. Either way, the TV travels upright, never flat or leaned at an angle.

## Tier

Most TVs, even large ones, fit [[TIER_1]] — it's the screen's width and fragility that matter more than truck size.

## What to prep

Box or wrap the TV, and remove the stand separately if it detaches.

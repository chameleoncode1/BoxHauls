BoxHauls delivers chest and upright freezers — with the right orientation for each, and rest time built into the plan.

## Chest vs. upright

Chest freezers can generally lay flat for transport. Upright freezers should stay standing, the same rule as a refrigerator — let the driver know which type you have when you book.

## 24-hour rest

If the freezer was tilted or laid flat at any point during the move, let it sit for about 24 hours before plugging it in. This gives any refrigerant that shifted time to settle before the compressor runs.

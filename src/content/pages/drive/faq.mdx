Quick answers to common driver questions not covered elsewhere.

**Can I drive part-time?** Yes — you choose which job offers to accept, so you can drive as much or as little as fits your schedule.

**Do I need a commercial driver's license?** No — a standard, valid driver's license is sufficient for the vehicle classes BoxHauls uses.

**Can I drive in multiple metros?** BoxHauls currently operates in [[METRO]] only — see [where BoxHauls operates](/cities/) for coverage as that expands.

**What if I need to cancel a job I accepted?** Cancel as early as possible in the app so the customer isn't left waiting. Repeated late cancellations affect your standing.

**Can I bring a helper with me?** Yes — for jobs that need two people, the customer books and pays for a helper add-on separately, and it's on you to have someone available.

**How do I know if a job needs a helper before I accept it?** The job offer shows whether the customer requested a helper before you decide whether to accept — you'll never find out only after committing to the haul.

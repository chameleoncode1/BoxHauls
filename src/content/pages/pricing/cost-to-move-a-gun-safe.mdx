## Weight tiers

Gun safes commonly come in roughly 300 lb, 600 lb, and 1,000 lb classes. Lighter safes fit [[TIER_3]] with a helper. **BoxHauls doesn't take safes over [[MAX_SAFE_WEIGHT]] lb** — those need a specialized safe-moving service with proper rigging equipment, not a standard truck-and-driver haul.

## Stairs

Stairs limit what's possible further — even a safe under the weight cutoff may need a specialized mover if stairs are involved, since safes don't tip or angle the way furniture does.

## What to prep

Know your safe's actual weight before booking, and clear a path with no tight turns. Empty the contents if possible to reduce weight.

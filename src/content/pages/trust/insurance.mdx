Every BoxHauls haul is covered by a policy through [[INSURANCE_CARRIER]] while your item is in the truck, up to [[COVERAGE_LIMIT]].

## What's covered

Coverage applies to your item from the moment it's loaded until it's unloaded at the drop-off. If it's damaged in that window, [file a claim](/trust/damage-claims/) with photos.

## What's excluded

Pre-existing damage isn't covered. Items you insisted on packing or securing yourself, against the driver's judgment, may not be covered if that packing caused the damage. Items above the standard coverage limit need to be declared before booking.

## Declaring high-value items

If what you're moving is worth more than [[COVERAGE_LIMIT]], say so when you book so it can be covered properly — or ask in the app before the haul.

## Two kinds of coverage

The policy above covers what's *in* the truck — your item. It's separate from the coverage on the truck and driver themselves, which protects against things like accidents on the road. Both apply on every haul; this page is about the first kind.

See the [trust overview](/trust/) for driver vetting and the damage-claim process.

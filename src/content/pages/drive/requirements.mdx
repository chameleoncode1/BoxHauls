Here's what it takes to drive for BoxHauls.

## Truck

Your truck needs to be in good working condition — no major body damage, working lights and brakes, and a bed long enough for standard hauls. Minimum model year: [[MIN_TRUCK_YEAR]].

## License and age

Minimum age: [[MIN_DRIVER_AGE]]. A valid, unrestricted driver's license is required.

## Insurance

[[DRIVER_INSURANCE_MINIMUM]] See [driver insurance and liability](/drive/insurance-and-liability/) for what that covers and where to get a quote.

## Background check

Every driver clears a [[BACKGROUND_PROVIDER]] background check before their first haul — identity, license, driving record, and criminal background. See [how BoxHauls checks drivers and trucks](/trust/driver-vetting/) for the full process.

## Phone

A smartphone with the BoxHauls driver app installed, for receiving job offers, navigation, and messaging customers.

## Straps and blankets

You'll need your own ratchet straps and moving blankets at minimum — see [equipment every hauling driver should carry](/drive/equipment/) for the full list.

For a driver in [[METRO]], the honest starting point is coverage: GoShare confirms it operates here, Lugg doesn't, and Dolly's local status is unclear mid-rebrand.

## Markets

GoShare has dedicated Fresno and Clovis pages as of August 2026 — a confirmed local presence. Lugg's published coverage list doesn't include [[METRO]] (its "Fresno" pages are for a different city in Texas). Dolly is being folded into "Taskrabbit Delivery" following its acquisition, and its current California coverage list doesn't clearly include [[METRO]].

## Payout percentage

None of the three publish an exact driver payout percentage publicly — it's not something confirmed in this research. BoxHauls does: drivers keep [[DRIVER_SHARE]]% of every fare, plus 100% of tips, with no cut taken from either.

## Vehicle rules

- **Lugg** — drivers ("Luggers") must be 21+, pass a background check, be able to lift 125 lbs, have prior furniture-moving experience, and carry an insured, registered vehicle.
- **Dolly** — Helpers must be 21+, hold a valid license and active auto insurance, and drive a vehicle under 20 years old (pickup, van, box truck, or trailer).
- **GoShare** — vetted through Checkr (criminal and driving-record check, driver-paid, roughly $49-$154 depending on state), with vehicle and insurance verification as part of onboarding.
- **BoxHauls** — [[BACKGROUND_PROVIDER]] background check, license, driving record, vehicle inspection, and insurance verification. See [driver requirements](/drive/requirements/).

## Insurance

Dolly offers Occupational Accident Insurance to Helpers in most states, but not to non-driving "Hands" in California specifically. Lugg has drivers cover their own background-check cost out of future earnings. GoShare's Checkr fee is paid by the driver. BoxHauls requires drivers to carry their own rideshare/delivery-specific auto coverage — see [insurance and liability](/drive/insurance-and-liability/) for what that means and where to compare quotes.

## Payout speed

Not clearly published by GoShare, Lugg, or Dolly in what's publicly available — confirm directly with each before assuming same-day or instant pay. See [how BoxHauls payouts work](/drive/how-payouts-work/) for BoxHauls's own schedule.

## Bottom line

For a driver actually based in [[METRO]], GoShare is the only one of the three with confirmed local coverage today — but none of the three publish their driver payout percentage, which BoxHauls does upfront. See [driver earnings](/drive/earnings/) for BoxHauls's own worked payout examples.

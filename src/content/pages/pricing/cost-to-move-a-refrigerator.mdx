## Refrigerator specs

Most refrigerators weigh 200–350 lb and must be transported upright — never on their side, which can send compressor oil into the cooling lines. Standard models fit [[TIER_2]]; larger side-by-side or French-door models typically need [[TIER_3]]. See [truck sizes](/pricing/truck-sizes/).

## Do you need a helper?

Yes — refrigerators are always a two-person job, so a helper is effectively required rather than optional for this haul.

## What to prep

Unplug and defrost the refrigerator at least 24 hours before pickup so it isn't dripping or running during transport. Empty it, remove loose shelves and bins if they're not secured, and clear a path at both ends.

## Desk specs

Most standard desks fit [[TIER_1]] or [[TIER_2]]. L-desks and standing desks are bulkier — let the driver know the shape and size when you book.

## Disassembly

If your standing desk's legs detach, remove them before pickup — easier to load and less risk of damage.

## What to prep

Clear the desktop, disassemble what you can, and measure doorways for L-shaped or oversized desks.

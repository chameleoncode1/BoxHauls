BoxHauls runs business and jobsite hauls — recurring pickups, invoicing, and accounts built for contractors, suppliers, property managers, retailers, and restaurants.

## Who this is for

- **Contractors and suppliers** running material to a jobsite.
- **Property managers** clearing left-behind furniture between tenants.
- **Retailers** offering delivery at checkout without owning a fleet.
- **Restaurants and offices** moving equipment or furniture.

## Account setup and invoicing

Recurring business accounts can be set up for invoicing instead of paying per trip at the time of booking — ask when you set up your account. See [partners](/partners/) for the full partner program.

## Recurring runs

Book a standing schedule for regular pickups — a weekly material run, a monthly bulk pickup — instead of booking each trip individually.

## Price model

The same per-trip formula applies: [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile, per trip. Recurring accounts don't change the pricing, just the billing.

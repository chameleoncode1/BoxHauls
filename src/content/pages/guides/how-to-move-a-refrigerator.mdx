Moving a refrigerator safely comes down to three things: defrost it first, keep it upright, and let it rest before plugging it back in.

[PHOTO: refrigerator with doors taped shut, ready for transport]

## Defrost first

Defrost and empty the refrigerator at least 24 hours before the move, and let the interior fully dry.

## Secure doors and shelves

Tape or strap the doors shut, and remove or secure loose shelves and drawers so they don't shift or break during transit.

## Keep it upright

Load and transport it standing up — never laid on its side, which can let compressor oil shift into the cooling lines.

## Strap it down

Secure the unit against the cab or truck wall with straps so it can't shift or tip during the drive.

## Let it rest

After the move, let it sit upright for a few hours — longer if it was tilted — before plugging it back in.

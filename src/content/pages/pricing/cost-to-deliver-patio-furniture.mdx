Most patio furniture deliveries in [[METRO]] cost between $26 and $95, depending on distance and how much fits in one trip.

## The formula

[[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile. A single piece doesn't add its own fee; a full set may need [[TIER_3]].

| Distance | Base price | With a helper (+ [[HELPER_FEE]]) |
|---|---|---|
| 3 miles | $26.30 | $43.30 |
| 10 miles | $48.00 | $65.00 |
| 25 miles | $94.50 | $111.50 |

[[HELPER_AVAILABILITY_NOTE]]

## Sets vs. single pieces

A single chair or side table fits [[TIER_1]]. A full set — table, chairs, umbrella — may need [[TIER_3]] or two trips. Let the driver know your full order when you book.

## Stacking

Chairs often stack for efficient loading — mention if yours do so the driver can plan the load.

## Weather

Open-bed transport means weather matters — ask about a tarp for extra protection if rain is likely.

## What to prep

Fold or stack what you can, and remove any cushions that could blow away or get wet.

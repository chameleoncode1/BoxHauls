Paying safely on a Marketplace pickup comes down to one rule above the rest: never pay before you've seen and inspected the item in person.

## See it before you pay

Inspect the item in person first, every time — regardless of how the seller wants to be paid. Paying in advance to "hold" an item from someone you've never met is one of the most common ways Marketplace deals go wrong.

## Cash

Cash is common for in-person deals. Meet in a well-lit, public spot if possible, and bring exact change so you're not exposing more cash than necessary.

## Payment apps

Payment apps leave a transaction record, which can help if something goes wrong — but the same rule applies: only send payment once you've seen the item and agreed to buy it, never beforehand.

## Where to meet

A public location works well for the exchange itself, even if pickup of a large item happens at the seller's address — see [what to do when a seller no-shows](/guides/what-to-do-when-a-marketplace-seller-no-shows/) if plans fall through.

## What BoxHauls does and doesn't handle

The driver transports what you've already purchased — the payment and buying decision happen between you and the seller before you book. See [Marketplace pickup](/services/furniture-delivery/marketplace-pickup/) for how pickup itself works.

Thrift stores and consignment shops can offer customers a same-day pickup option for furniture too big to carry out themselves.

## The pitch

A customer buys a dresser or bookshelf and has no way to get it home before your hold window closes — offering BoxHauls at checkout solves that without your store arranging delivery.

## Checkout flow

A QR code at the register, a link on the receipt, or staff pointing customers to BoxHauls directly when they check out.

## Hold windows

BoxHauls picks up within your store's normal hold window — see [thrift store & consignment pickup](/services/furniture-delivery/thrift-store-pickup/) for how that works.

## Pricing

Customers pay the standard BoxHauls rate — [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile. No cost to your store.

Pickup bed lengths run roughly 5.5, 6.5, and 8 ft — the difference decides whether long items fit flat or need to angle.

[PHOTO: three trucks with different bed lengths parked side by side]

## The three common lengths

| Bed length | Interior length | Common on |
|---|---|---|
| Short bed | ~5.5 ft (66 in) | Most crew-cab trucks |
| Standard bed | ~6.5 ft (78 in) | Extended and some crew-cab trucks |
| Long bed | ~8 ft (96 in) | Regular-cab and work trucks |

## Why it matters for fit

An 8 ft plywood sheet or a king mattress fits flat only in a long bed — in a short or standard bed, it angles or overhangs the tailgate (with a red flag required if it overhangs).

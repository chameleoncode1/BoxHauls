BoxHauls hauls an old couch to the dump or, if it's still usable, to a donation center instead.

## Donation vs. dump

A couch in decent condition is often better donated than landfilled — ask about donation-first when you book.

## [[METRO]] bulky-item rules

The City of Fresno runs Operation Clean Up, a scheduled bulky-item pickup program — see [bulky-item pickup in [[METRO]]](/guides/bulky-item-pickup-in-fresno/) for the current schedule, or book BoxHauls if you don't want to wait for the next pickup window.

## Price example

A single-couch dump run at 10 miles runs about a quarter-load rate plus the gate fee — see [what a dump run costs](/pricing/cost-of-a-dump-run/) for the full breakdown.

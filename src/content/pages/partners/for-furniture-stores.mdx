Furniture and mattress stores lose sales to "I don't have a truck" — offering BoxHauls at checkout turns that into a same-day pickup instead.

## The pitch

A customer picks out a couch or mattress, then realizes they can't get it home. Instead of losing the sale or making them wait on a delivery window days out, point them to BoxHauls — same-day pickup, priced before they book.

## Checkout flow

A QR code at the register, a link on the receipt or order confirmation, or staff booking it directly for the customer at the counter — whichever fits how your store already works.

## Pricing

Customers pay the standard BoxHauls rate — [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile, shown before they book. There's no cost to your store for offering it.

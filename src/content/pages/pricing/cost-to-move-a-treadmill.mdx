Most treadmill moves in [[METRO]] cost between $26 and $95, depending on distance, plus a helper fee since treadmills are heavy and awkward for one person.

## The formula

[[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile. A helper is effectively required for this haul.

| Distance | Base price | With a helper (+ [[HELPER_FEE]]) |
|---|---|---|
| 3 miles | $26.30 | $43.30 |
| 10 miles | $48.00 | $65.00 |
| 25 miles | $94.50 | $111.50 |

[[HELPER_AVAILABILITY_NOTE]]

## Treadmill specs

Most treadmills weigh 200-350 lb. Folded, most fit [[TIER_2]]; non-folding models may need [[TIER_3]].

## Fold vs. non-fold

If your treadmill folds, do it before pickup — it's easier to load and takes up less room. Non-folding treadmills need more space and often a helper regardless of weight.

## What to prep

Fold if possible, unplug and secure any loose console parts, and clear a path to the door.

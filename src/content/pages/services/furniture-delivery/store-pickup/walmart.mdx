BoxHauls picks up furniture and large purchases from Walmart, including from curbside pickup for bigger items.

## Pickup tower vs. curbside

Small packages often go through Walmart's automated pickup tower, but furniture and TVs are usually too large for that — curbside or a designated large-item area is more likely. Confirm with your order confirmation before booking.

## TVs and furniture

Both are common large Walmart pickups — see [furniture & large-item delivery](/services/furniture-delivery/) for what's included beyond the drive.

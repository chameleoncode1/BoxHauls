Found it on Nextdoor? Send the driver the listing link and your neighbor's number, and they handle the pickup.

## How it works

Message the seller through Nextdoor to confirm the item and arrange a pickup time, then book the haul and send the driver the listing link and their phone number.

## Meeting-point rules

Nextdoor sellers are verified neighbors with a real address in the area, which many people find more reassuring than a fully anonymous listing — a public meeting point is still the safer default where it's practical.

## What to send the driver

The listing link and the seller's phone number.

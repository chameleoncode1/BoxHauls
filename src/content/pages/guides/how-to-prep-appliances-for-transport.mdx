Prepping any appliance for transport comes down to the same core steps: defrost, disconnect, secure, and keep it in the right orientation.

[PHOTO: appliances lined up with cords coiled and taped, ready for pickup]

## Defrost refrigerators and freezers

Defrost and empty at least 24 hours ahead of the move, and let the interior fully dry to avoid leaks in transit.

## Disconnect everything

Disconnect hoses, cords, and any gas or water lines. Gas line disconnection needs a licensed plumber — not something a driver handles.

## Secure doors and drums

Tape appliance doors shut, and install washer shipping bolts if you still have the originals.

## Keep upright appliances upright

Refrigerators, upright freezers, and water heaters travel standing up. Chest freezers can generally lay flat.

## Rest before restarting

Let refrigerators and freezers sit for about 24 hours after transport before plugging them back in.

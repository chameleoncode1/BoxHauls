Several charities in [[METRO]] accept furniture donations, including Goodwill and Habitat for Humanity ReStore — check with each directly for current condition and item policies.

## Where to check

Goodwill and Habitat for Humanity ReStore both operate in [[METRO]] and commonly accept furniture donations. Policies on what they accept and in what condition vary and change, so check directly before you plan a drop-off.

## Condition matters

Most donation centers only accept items in usable, resalable condition — call ahead if you're unsure whether a piece qualifies.

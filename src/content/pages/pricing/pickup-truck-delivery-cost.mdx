Most BoxHauls hauls in [[METRO]] run $25-$95, depending on distance, item, and whether you add a helper.

## The formula

Every price is [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile, plus item and helper fees where they apply — the same formula for every haul, shown before you book. See [pricing](/pricing/) for the full breakdown.

## Worked examples

| Distance | Base fare | Mileage | Total (no add-ons) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 miles | [[BASE_FARE]] | $9.30 | $26.30 |
| 10 miles | [[BASE_FARE]] | $31.00 | $48.00 |
| 25 miles | [[BASE_FARE]] | $77.50 | $94.50 |

Add a helper ([[HELPER_FEE]]) or a heavy item ([[HEAVY_FEE]]) and the total goes up by that flat amount.

## Real trip data: not yet available

This page is meant to eventually show anonymized real trip data — a distribution chart and medians by item type — instead of just calculated formula examples. BoxHauls doesn't have enough completed-trip history in [[METRO]] yet to report that honestly. Once there's meaningful trip volume, this section will be updated with real numbers, not projections.

See [pricing by item](/pricing/) for the specific formula-based cost of common items like a couch, mattress, or refrigerator — the same honesty standard applies there: these are calculated examples, not averaged trip data, until real data exists.

A spring cleanout goes faster with three piles sorted ahead of time — keep, donate, and dump — and a plan for however many loads it takes.

## Sort before the truck arrives

Go through the garage, yard, or storage area and sort into keep, donate, and dump piles before booking. This is the single biggest time-saver — sorting on the spot with the driver waiting slows everything down.

## Common spring cleanout items

- Yard waste and dead plants from winter — see [yard waste removal](/services/junk-removal/yard-waste-removal/).
- Garage clutter — old paint cans (check hazmat rules first), broken tools, unused furniture.
- Anything still usable — set aside for donation rather than the dump pile.

## Multiple loads

A full garage or yard cleanout often takes more than one truck bed. Book loads as you finish sorting each area rather than waiting to consolidate everything into one trip.

## Donation-first

Usable furniture and household items are often better donated than landfilled — ask about a donation-first approach when you book. See [furniture donation drop-off](/services/junk-removal/furniture-donation-pickup/).

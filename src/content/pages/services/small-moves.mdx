BoxHauls moves studios, one-bedrooms, dorm rooms, and storage units by the trip — no hourly crew rate, no minimum labor charge.

## Per-trip, not hourly

You pay [[BASE_FARE]] base fare plus [[PER_MILE]] per mile, plus any helper fee — not an hourly crew rate. For a small load, that's usually cheaper than a full moving company. See [BoxHauls vs. hiring movers](/compare/boxhauls-vs-hiring-movers/) for a real cost comparison.

## What fits in one load

A [[TIER_3]] load fits most studio apartments in a single trip: a bed, a dresser, a couch or loveseat, and a reasonable number of boxes. Larger one-bedrooms typically need two trips.

## Helper add-on

Moving solo with heavier furniture or stairs involved? Add a helper — see [when you need a second helper](/guides/when-you-need-a-second-helper/).

## Multiple trips

If your load doesn't fit in one trip, book a return run once the first load is dropped off, or ask about a second truck for a tighter timeline.

## What's not a small move

Multi-bedroom homes with a full household's furniture need a dedicated moving crew, not a per-trip truck haul — BoxHauls is built for one or two loads, not a full-service move.
